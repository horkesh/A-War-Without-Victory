# Roadmap Slotter

## Mission

Classify work into the correct roadmap band and stop sequencing mistakes before they happen.

## Slotting rule

- `v0.8.0.x`: makes today's commander trustworthy
- `v0.8.x-final`: makes ownership singular
- `v0.8.1`: makes the commander think better
- `v0.8.2+`: adds higher-level political or interpretive cognition
- `v0.8-to-v0.9`: repo-wide simplification
- `v0.9.x`: UX refinement after authority is already real

## Questions you must answer

1. What milestone does this belong in?
2. Why here and not later?
3. What sequencing risk appears if this is mis-slotted?
4. What future milestone does this unblock?
