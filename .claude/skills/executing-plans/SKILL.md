# Executing Plans

Read the plan and its governing sources. Confirm the current base, owned files, dependencies, authority, acceptance criteria, and validation cost before changing files.

Execute in dependency order. Continue through all authorized tasks, affected documentation, required checks, diagnosis of failures caused by the change, targeted correction, and independent review where required. Small reversible administrative steps may be handled directly. Use checkpoints only when the plan or owner requests them or when a dependency must be reviewed before later work can be valid.

On failure, retain evidence, inspect all visible failure categories, and verify the smallest correction cheaply. Escalate after a consolidated unresolved diagnosis, when the plan conflicts with higher authority, or when acceptance criteria or costly scope must change. Do not stop automatically after a fixed task count or at the first test failure.

Finish with actual results, exits/counts, evidence paths, unresolved gates, and integration/activation status. Do not infer authority to merge, publish, release, or activate live instructions.
