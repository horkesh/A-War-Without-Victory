# Mediator Trust: The Channel That Was Not Burned

**Ring 2 — narrative observation. Path-not-taken. Ghost entry.**

The international mediation architecture across the war passed through identifiable phases. *Balkan Battlegrounds* II Chapters 31 and 51 describe the institutional sequence: the European Community Conference on Yugoslavia under Lord Carrington, the International Conference on the Former Yugoslavia under Cyrus Vance and David Owen, the Owen–Stoltenberg framework, the Contact Group plan of July 1994, and the Holbrooke shuttle that produced the Dayton General Framework Agreement of 14 December 1995. Each phase was conditioned by the trust the parties extended to the mediating institutions and to the individuals carrying the briefs. The historical record contains documented withdrawals of trust at every stage — refusals to receive mediators, public denunciations of named negotiators, declarations that named bodies were not impartial — and the consequences those withdrawals had on what could be negotiated and when.

In this campaign, on the player faction's track, the mediator-trust streak counter advanced across the recorded turn window without registering a denounce-mediator event, a refuse-reception event, or a declared-partial-mediator event on the player faction's ledger. By the audit turn, the trust channel to the mediating body for the active phase remained open by the run's diplomatic ledger reading.

The historical denunciations stand. The Pale Assembly's posture toward the Vance–Owen plan, the Sarajevo Presidency's posture toward the Owen–Stoltenberg framework, the Mostar leadership's posture toward the central-Bosnia local mediation track — these are recorded in the canonical political Codex. This entry does not displace them.

What is recorded here is operational: the trust channel, asked many times across many turns whether it would be repudiated, was not repudiated within the window. Whether the channel was kept open by conviction, by tactical calculation, or by absence of a precipitating provocation is not adjudicated here; the streak counter advanced, and the audit reading registers that fact.
