# The Caucus That Did Not Crack

**Ring 2 — narrative observation. Path-not-taken. Ghost entry.**

Each of the three principal political-military leaderships of the war operated under documented internal pressure. Sarajevo's wartime Presidency contained competing factions on questions of survival posture, alliance management, and acceptable peace terms. The Pale–Banja Luka axis carried persistent strain between the political leadership and the General Staff and between the western Krajina and eastern Drina-corridor wings of the entity. The Mostar leadership of the Croatian Community of Herceg-Bosna operated under cross-pressure from Zagreb and from local political dynamics in central Bosnia and Herzegovina proper. The pattern documented in *Balkan Battlegrounds* II Chapters 31 and 51, the Trial Chamber findings of *Prosecutor v. Karadžić* (IT-95-5/18-T) and *Prosecutor v. Prlić et al.* (IT-04-74-T), and the contemporary press coverage shows recurrent caucus rifts under the joint pressure of war exhaustion, casualty accumulation, and erosion of internal cohesion.

In this campaign, on the player faction's track, the joint predicate that triggers a temporary political split — the simultaneous crossing of the alliance-strain threshold, the cumulative-casualties audit threshold, and the international-standing audit threshold — was not satisfied within the recorded turn window. The political-split-temporary flag did not register on the player faction's ledger by the audit turn. The cabinet held; the caucus, however reluctantly, held.

The historical record on the documented rifts — Pale Assembly disputes, Sarajevo Presidency factional realignments, Herceg-Bosna leadership disagreements — remains in the canonical Codex. Those rifts occurred. This entry does not displace them.

What is recorded here is narrow: by the audit thresholds and within the turn window in which the historical pattern would normally have fired, on the player faction's specific track, the joint condition was not met, and the cohesion-and-credibility cost the cost-ledger annotation describes was not levied.
