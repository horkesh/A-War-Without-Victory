Galić* (IT-98-29-T) and *Prosecutor v. Dragomir Milošević* (IT-98-29/1-T) record the ceasefire context against which the Sarajevo siege violations were judged. The pattern is one of agreements honoured intermittently and locally, broken under operational pressure, and renewed under international auspices.
