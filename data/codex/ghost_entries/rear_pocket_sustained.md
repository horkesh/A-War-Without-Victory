The Trial Chambers addressed the pattern in detail. *Prosecutor v. Tadić* (IT-94-1-T), *Prosecutor v. Stakić* (IT-97-24-T), and the Prijedor-municipality cases established the factual record of what rear-area authority was used to do in territory under sustained Republika Srpska control. *Prosecutor v. Prlić et al.* (IT-04-74-T) addressed the analogous pattern in territory under Croatian Defence Council authority. The factual record is settled; the convictions stand.
