# Force Quality: The Inversion That History Did Not Show

**Ring 2 — narrative observation. Path-not-taken. Ghost entry. CoS analytical assessment.**

For most of the war, per-brigade combat power on the Army of Republika Srpska side exceeded that of the Army of the Republic of Bosnia and Herzegovina by a substantial margin. The reasons are documented. The VRS inherited the bulk of the Yugoslav People's Army's deployed equipment, depots, command-and-control infrastructure, and trained personnel within Bosnia and Herzegovina at the moment of the JNA's formal withdrawal in May 1992; the *Mladić* trial chamber in *Prosecutor v. Mladić* (IT-09-92-T) entered findings on the formation of the VRS from those JNA components. The personnel-centre mechanism documented in *Prosecutor v. Perišić* (IT-04-81-T) sustained that material and human continuity through the war's middle years. UN Security Council Resolution 713, adopted in September 1991, imposed an arms embargo on the territory of the former Yugoslavia that, in operation, locked in the asymmetry: the side already in possession of the JNA's arsenal kept its advantage, and the side reliant on outside resupply faced a blockade.

In this campaign, that asymmetry inverted. For at least eight consecutive turns of the run, per-brigade combat power on the Republika Srpska side fell below that of the player faction. The inversion was sustained — not a single anomalous turn — and persisted long enough to register as a strategic feature rather than a measurement artefact.

The mechanism that produced the inversion is recorded in the run's order-of-battle ledgers and in the per-brigade attrition, replacement, and reinforcement tables. The historical drivers of the asymmetry — the embargo, the JNA inheritance, the patron supply line — are unchanged in their canonical narrative form. What changed in this campaign was the degree to which they translated into sustained combat advantage.

This entry records the inversion. It does not claim that the inversion altered the war's outcome, prevented atrocity, or produced any other consequence beyond what the run's own ledgers record.
