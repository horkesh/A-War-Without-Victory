# Civilian Displacement: The Wave That Did Not Crest

**Ring 2 — narrative observation. Path-not-taken. Ghost entry.**

The Bosnian War produced the largest civilian displacement in Europe since the Second World War. The UNHCR registered about 2.2 million displaced persons by 1995, divided between internally displaced persons within Bosnia and Herzegovina and refugees abroad. *Balkan Battlegrounds* I and II document the displacement waves: the spring–summer 1992 displacement out of the Republika Srpska–claimed municipalities, the central-Bosnia displacement of 1992–1993 between Bosniak and Croat populations, the Drina-valley enclave displacements that culminated in the events of July 1995, and the displacement of Croatian Serbs from the Krajina in Croatia during Operation Storm in August 1995, outside Bosnia and Herzegovina. The Trial Chambers in *Prosecutor v. Krajišnik* (IT-00-39-T) and the related Prijedor-municipality cases addressed forced displacement within the broader campaign of persecution.
