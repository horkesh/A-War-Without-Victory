# The Sustained Refusal: Paramilitary Authorisation Withheld Across the Run

**Ring 2 — narrative observation. Path-not-taken. Ghost entry.**

In the historical war, the political-military pattern in which paramilitary detachments were authorised for rear-area work alongside regular formations was not an isolated decision; it was a sustained policy posture, renewed across many months and many municipalities. The Trial Chambers documented the pattern in detail. *Prosecutor v. Karadžić* (IT-95-5/18-T) and *Prosecutor v. Krajišnik* (IT-00-39-T) addressed the standing directives and joint criminal enterprise that integrated paramilitary capacity into the campaigns in the scheduled municipalities. *Prosecutor v. Lukić & Lukić* (IT-98-32/1-T) addressed the operations of the White Eagles in Višegrad. *Prosecutor v. Stanišić & Simatović* (IT-03-69-T, on appeal) addressed the State Security relationship to paramilitary deployment. The authorisation was not a single signature; it was a continuous one.

In this campaign, the corresponding political-military posture refused that authorisation continuously. The standing order was set to deny, the rear-pocket discipline held across the recorded turn window, and the clean-record divergence flag was set and not subsequently retracted. By the audit turn, the war crimes counter on the player faction's negotiation capital ledger remained at zero, and the absence of paramilitary deployment for the recorded turn window remained a continuous fact rather than a single refusal.

The historical convictions stand. The findings of the Tribunal on what was done in Višegrad, Foča, Prijedor, and the scheduled municipalities of the Karadžić indictment are not displaced by this entry. Nor does this entry suggest that the player faction's posture was equivalent to other factions' postures, or that other factions' atrocities were less grave than the historical record establishes.

What is recorded here is operational and narrow: the standing order, asked many times over many turns, was answered each time with a denial. The campaign of authorisations that the historical record documents was, at this command level and across this run, not begun.
