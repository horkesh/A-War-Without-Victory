# Paramilitary Authorisation Not Given Across the Run

**Ring 2 — narrative observation. Path-not-taken. Ghost entry.**

In the historical war, the political-military pattern in which paramilitary detachments were authorised for rear-area work alongside regular formations was not an isolated decision; it was a sustained policy posture, renewed across many months and many municipalities. The Trial Chambers documented the pattern in detail. *Prosecutor v. Karadžić* (IT-95-5/18-T) and *Prosecutor v. Krajišnik* (IT-00-39-T) addressed the standing directives and joint criminal enterprise that integrated paramilitary capacity into the campaigns in the scheduled municipalities. *Prosecutor v. Lukić & Lukić* (IT-98-32/1-T) addressed the operations of the White Eagles in Višegrad. *Prosecutor v. Stanišić & Simatović* (IT-03-69-T) addressed the State Security relationship to paramilitary deployment. The authorisation was not a single signature; it was a continuous one.

In this campaign, no paramilitary deployment was authorised by this command.

The historical convictions stand. The findings of the Tribunal on what was done in Višegrad, Foča, Prijedor, and the scheduled municipalities of the Karadžić indictment are not displaced by this entry. Nor does this entry suggest that the player faction's posture was equivalent to other factions' postures, or that other factions' atrocities were less grave than the historical record establishes.

What is recorded here is operational and narrow: no paramilitary deployment is recorded as authorised by this command. The campaign of authorisations that the historical record documents was, at this command level and across this run, not begun.
