# Paramilitary Deployment: No Authorisation Recorded

**Ring 2 — narrative observation. Path-not-taken. Ghost entry.**

In the historical war, campaigns of persecution, deportation and forcible transfer of civilians, commonly called ethnic cleansing, were carried out under common criminal plans. The Trial Chambers of the International Criminal Tribunal for the former Yugoslavia documented their execution across the Drina valley, Prijedor, the Lašva valley, Mostar, and beyond, in judgments including *Prosecutor v. Karadžić* (IT-95-5/18-T), *Prosecutor v. Krajišnik* (IT-00-39-T), *Prosecutor v. Lukić & Lukić* (IT-98-32/1-T) on Višegrad, *Prosecutor v. Kunarac et al.* (IT-96-23-T & IT-96-23/1-T) on the rape and enslavement of Bosniak women and girls in Foča, *Prosecutor v. Kordić & Čerkez* (IT-95-14/2-T) on the Lašva valley, and *Prosecutor v. Prlić et al.* (IT-04-74-T) on the joint criminal enterprise in Herceg-Bosna. Paramilitary formations — Arkan's Tigers, the White Eagles, and others — operated in coordination with regular army and police units, conducting the killings, expulsions, sexual violence, and detention that gave the campaigns their particular character.

In this campaign, the player faction's standing paramilitary policy reads as refusal. No paramilitary deployment is recorded as authorised by this command. The war crimes counter for the player faction reads zero.

The historical convictions stand. The Tribunal's findings on what was done in Višegrad, Foča, Prijedor, Ahmići (*Kordić & Čerkez*), Stupni Do (*Prosecutor v. Rajić*, IT-95-12-S), and the scheduled municipalities of the Karadžić indictment remain in the record. This entry does not relativise those crimes, suggest that other factions' atrocities were less grave, or claim a moral comparison favourable to the player.

What is recorded here is the narrow operational fact: no paramilitary deployment is recorded as authorised by this command, and the standing policy reads as refusal. The deployment of paramilitary formations that ICTY judgments document as part of common criminal plans was not, at this command level, authorised.
