# Eastern Enclaves: Audit-Only Situation Report

**Ring 2 — narrative observation. AUDIT-ONLY. Present-state register only — UNHCR situation-report voice. This entry does not address Srebrenica 1995 in any causal or counterfactual register.**

This entry records, in the register of a situation report, the present operational status of the three eastern Bosnian Muslim enclaves at the recorded turn N. As of that turn, the Army of the Republic of Bosnia and Herzegovina retained control of the operational settlements at Srebrenica, Žepa, and Goražde. Garrison units are listed in the order of battle. Civilian populations remain in place under siege conditions. Humanitarian access is contested. Lines of supply, where they exist, run through UNPROFOR-mediated corridors and clandestine routes through the surrounding Drina Corps zone of operations. None of the three enclaves had, as of turn N, been overrun.

This is the operational picture at the snapshot turn. It is not a forecast. It is not an outcome. It is not a comparison.

The historical record stands and is not displaced by anything observed here. In July 1995, the Srebrenica safe area fell to forces under the command of the Army of Republika Srpska. The Trial Chamber in *Prosecutor v. Krstić* (IT-98-33-T) found that genocide was committed in the days that followed, against the Bosnian Muslim men and boys of Srebrenica. That finding was upheld on appeal. *Prosecutor v. Karadžić* (IT-95-5/18-T) and *Prosecutor v. Mladić* (IT-09-92-T) entered convictions consistent with that determination at the level of command responsibility. The International Court of Justice, in *Bosnia and Herzegovina v. Serbia and Montenegro* (2007), found that the events at Srebrenica constituted genocide as defined in the 1948 Convention. Approximately 8,372 Bosniak men and boys were killed. Žepa fell shortly after Srebrenica; its population was expelled. Goražde survived through 1995 only with NATO and UNPROFOR intervention.

Those judgments are facts of the historical record. They remain so. This audit notes the present state at turn N within the campaign and nothing further. It draws no inference about subsequent turns, and it draws no comparison between this campaign and the events the Tribunal and the ICJ adjudicated.

The full record of those events is held in the historical Codex entries on the Srebrenica arc. They are not displaced by this audit and should be read in their canonical form.
