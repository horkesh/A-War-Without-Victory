Equipment: 60 tanks, 30 armored vehicles (26 tanks on 2 Jun 1992 incl 10 M-84)
Strength: Formation 25,940; actual 16,805 (Jun 1992, 65%); fell to ~15,000 (58%) in 1994
Casualties: 2,000+ KIA (637 KIA by Aug 1994; most losses in autumn 1994 and 1995)
JNA predecessor: 9th Knin Corps + 10th Zagreb Corps (both evacuated from Croatia)
AoR: 16 western Krajina municipalities
Postwar: Disbanded Jan 1996; units absorbed into 1KK

Initially known as "Drvarski korpus" before 12 May 1992

## KEY FINDINGS AND CROSS-REFERENCES

### SRK Initial -> Final (13 -> 7-8):
- Initial 13: 1.smehbr, 2.slpbr, Ilidza, Ilijas, Blazuj, Novo Sarajevo, Kosevska, Rajlovac, Vogosca, 1.rompbr, 2.rommtbr, Rogatica, 4.slpbr (Pale)
- Transfers out: 2.rommtbr -> DK (Nov 92), Rogatica -> DK (Nov 92)
- Disbanded: Novo Sarajevo (Jun 92)
- Renamed: Blazuj -> Igman (May 93)
- Merged: Kosevska + Rajlovac + Vogosca -> 3rd Sarajevo (Feb 94)
- Final 7-8: 1.smehbr, 2.slpbr, 3.spbr, 4.slpbr, 1.rompbr, Ilidza, Ilijas, Igman

### Drina Corps formation (Nov 1992):
From EBC: 1. zvornick, 1. bircanska
From SRK: 1. podrinjska (Rogatica), 2. romanijka
From HK: 2. podrinjska (Visegrad), 3. podrinjska (Cajnice), 4. podrinjska (Rudo), 5. podrinjska (Gorazde)
Newly formed: Bratunac (Nov 92), Milici-Vlasenica (Jan 93, later split), SPB Skelani (Dec 92)
Mid-1993/1994: 3rd + 4th Podrinje returned to HK -> merged into 14th HK bde

### Herzegovina mergers:
- May 1993: 3rd HK bde formed (Kalinovik)
- Aug 1994: 11th HK bde formed (Foca) from 1st + 2nd Foca bdes + TG
- Jul 1994: 14th HK bde formed from 3rd + 4th Podrinje (ex-DK)
- 1st Guards mtbr attached to HK from 1994

### 2KK transfers:
- Nov 1994: 7. kupres mtbr transferred to 1KK
- Nov 1994: 1. novogradska pbr attached to 2KK
- Late 1994: 1. srpska + 3. srpska bdes (GS level) deployed with 2KK
- Studentska brigada sent to Bihac front late 1994

### Notable unit facts:
- 3rd Semberija bde had a Bosniak commander (Pasaga Halilovic) — unique in VRS
- 5th Kozara bde was mobilized in 1991 for Western Slavonia (Croatia war)
- 1st Osinja bde started as Partizan detachment on 9 Feb 1992 — one of earliest VRS formations
- Gatacka brigada (18th HK): 2,500+ km warpath, never lost a position, only 51 KIA
- 1st Romanija bde: 15,000 men passed through during war
- 65th Protection Regiment: awarded both Orden Nemanjica and Orden Milosa Obilica (only unit with both)
