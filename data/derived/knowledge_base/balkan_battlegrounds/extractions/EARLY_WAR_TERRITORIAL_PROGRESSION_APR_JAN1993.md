## 1. Operation Corridor 92 — Full Timeline

**Duration:** "a large operation lasting **over six months**" (BB1 p.177)

### Prelude (March–May 1992)
- **Early March 1992:** First clashes at Bosanski Brod. HVO/HV forces stop Serb takeover (BB1 p.181)
- **April–May 1992:** HV reinforcements arrive. HVO/HV push JNA/Serb TO **out of** Bosanski Brod, **overrun Modriča and Derventa**. East-west route severed. RS "cleft in two" (BB1 p.181)
- VRS/JNA manage to hold flanking positions at **Doboj, Bosanski Šamac-Pelagićevo, and Brčko** (BB1 p.182)

### Phase 1: Opening the Corridor (June 1992)
- **Early June:** 1st Krajina Corps under General Momir Talić begins preliminary operations between Doboj and Derventa (BB1 p.182)
- **20 June:** Fall of Kotorsko and Johovac (north of Doboj) clears the path (BB1 p.182)
- **24 June:** 1st Krajina Corps launches **Operation "Corridor 92"** — first phase, attempting to break through to East Bosnian Corps NE of Gradačac (BB1 p.182)
- **26 June:** Two days of heavy fighting; units link up, cut Modriča-Gradačac connection (BB1 p.182)
- **28 June:** Modriča falls to VRS (BB1 p.182)

### Phase 2: Expanding (July 1992)
- **4 July:** Three-pronged VRS attack toward Derventa, Bosanski Brod, Odžak, and the Sava River (BB1 p.182)
- **4–5 July:** VRS retakes Derventa (BB1 p.182)
- **12 July:** Odžak seized (BB1 p.182)
- **14 July:** VRS reaches the Sava River (BB1 p.182)
- Other units push to within 10 km of Bosanski Brod. HV/HVO forced into constricted bridgehead around Bosanski Brod (BB1 p.182)

### Phase 3: Bosanski Brod & Securing (August–October 1992)
- **August–September:** Attacks around Bosanski Brod achieve only limited success (BB1 p.182)
- **Mid-September:** HVO and ARBiH troops **cut the corridor link south of Orašje**. VRS reopens it (BB1 p.182)
- **27 September:** VRS begins new attack on Bosanski Brod bridgehead — only small initial gains (BB1 p.182)
- **4 October:** VRS shifts attack sector, breaks through HV/HVO lines (BB1 p.182)
- **6 October:** **Bosanski Brod falls** (BB1 p.182)
- **Mid-October:** Corridor cut again temporarily (BB1 p.182)

### Phase 4: Failed Final Consolidation (November–December 1992)
- **Early November:** HVO units cut the road again (BB1 p.182)
- **November:** VRS offensive to clear Orašje pocket and secure route permanently **fails**. HV/HVO drive VRS back in heavy fighting and terrible weather (BB1 p.182)
- **Mid-to-late December:** 1st Krajina and East Bosnian Corps attack ARBiH 2nd Corps and HVO west of Brčko, gaining 2-3 km (BB1 p.182)
- **End of 1992:** "corridor remained a bare **3 kilometers wide** at its narrowest point, southwest of Brcko town. Widening this portion of the vital route would have to wait until 1993" (BB1 p.182)

## 3. Drina Valley — NOT "Cleared by May"

BB1 p.187 provides the definitive picture:

### What the VRS actually held:
- **Zvornik town** (9-10 April) + road to Šekovići (by mid-May)
- **Foča municipality** (April + mopping up)
- **Most of Rogatica municipality** (initial fighting)

### What the VRS did NOT hold:
- **Srebrenica**: "Serb TO troops had initially seized Srebrenica, but on **8-10 May** local Muslim forces, led by the charismatic Naser Oric, **drove out the Serbs**" (BB1 p.187). Orić then "steadily widening government control" through rest of 1992.
- **Cerska-Kamenica pocket**: Survived through 1992 into Feb-Mar 1993 (BB2 p.404, p.406)
- **Žepa pocket**: Survived to July 1995
- **Goražde**: "initial efforts to capture the town in May... came to naught" (BB1 p.187). ARBiH held it.
- **Trnovo**: ARBiH "major offensive in late July, seizing the passage to Gorazde and pushing VRS troops out of Trnovo" (BB1 p.187)
- **Visegrad environs**: "series of Bosnian Army attacks from late August to November retook key territory around Visegrad. This offensive placed ARBiH troops less than 3 kilometers from a key hydroelectric dam" (BB1 p.187)

### The Zvornik-Šekovići corridor was REPEATEDLY cut:
"they spent the rest of the year blocking repeated Muslim attempts to sever this route, **which succeeded at least three or four times**" (BB1 p.187)

**Verdict:** The Drina valley in 1992 was an active, contested theater with seesaw fighting. The VRS held the main towns but the ARBiH held substantial rural territory, multiple enclaves, and conducted successful counteroffensives. "Largely cleared by May" is flatly wrong.

## 4. VRS Operational Tempo 1992

From BB1 pp.177-197:

| Period | Tempo | BB Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| April 1992 | **Peak (blitz)** | Simultaneous seizure of towns across BiH. JNA/paramilitary shock operations. Zvornik, Foča, Prijedor (political), Doboj, Brčko, Srebrenica (temporarily) |
| May 1992 | **Very high** | Consolidation. Prijedor military ops (23 May). Srebrenica LOST to Orić (8-10 May). Drina road-opening. Sarajevo siege intensifies |
| June 1992 | **High** | Corridor 92 launched (24 June). Modriča retaken (28 June). Preliminary ops at Doboj-Derventa. Still offensive across multiple axes |
| July 1992 | **High but refocusing** | Derventa retaken (4-5 July). Odžak (12 July). Sava reached (14 July). Jajce preparations begin. VRS "redeploying some units" from Prijedor area to "more active sectors" (BB1 p.181) |
| August 1992 | **Moderate-high** | Jajce campaign "began in earnest in mid-August" (BB1 p.183). Corridor ops around Bosanski Brod achieving "only limited success" (BB1 p.182). Drina valley: ARBiH counteroffensives begin |
| September 1992 | **Moderate** | Jajce second phase "early/mid-September" (BB1 p.183). VRS attack on Bosanski Brod (27 Sept) — small gains. Corridor **cut** by HVO/ARBiH south of Orašje (BB1 p.182) |
| October 1992 | **Moderate, decisive results** | Bosanski Brod falls (6 Oct). Jajce falls (29 Oct). HVO-VRS ceasefire (9 Oct). But corridor cut again mid-October (BB1 p.182) |
| November 1992 | **Declining** | VRS November offensive to clear Orašje **fails** (BB1 p.182). Corridor cut again by HVO. Drina: ARBiH retaking territory near Višegrad |
| December 1992 | **Low** | Minor corridor widening near Brčko (2-3 km gain). Drina: Orić launches major offensive toward Bratunac/Skelani (BB1 p.187, BB2 p.404) |

**Key corrections from standup's oversimplified arc:**
1. RS was NOT at 45% by end-April — corridor was SEVERED, RS was in two pieces
2. Drina valley NOT cleared by May — Srebrenica recaptured by ARBiH on 8-10 May
3. Operation Corridor 92 launched 24 June, fighting through December (6+ months, not weeks)
4. RS peak ~63-68% reached only after Jajce fell (29 October), not by August
5. RS CONTRACTED slightly in Nov-Dec due to Orić's offensive and failed VRS November operations
6. Corridor remained only 3 km wide at narrowest through end of 1992

## 6. Implications for Sim Calibration

### RS w40 target should reflect:
- ~63-67% by area at January 1993
- Corridor extremely narrow (3 km) — sim should model this vulnerability
- Multiple ARBiH-held pockets/enclaves in Drina (Srebrenica expanding, Cerska, Žepa, Goražde)
- Jajce only fell at w28, not earlier
- Bihać pocket intact throughout

### The arc should show:
1. Rapid initial gains (April) but NOT reaching 45%+ until late May
2. **Plateau** in June while corridor opens (offensive resources concentrated)
3. Gradual gains July-October as Corridor, Jajce, and Bosanski Brod fall
4. **Slight decline or flat** November-January as VRS offensive tempo exhausts and ARBiH counterattacks

- n662: RS w40 = 0.518 (by OSID count = ~52%), area-weighted 89.9%
- Painted target: 55.4% by OSID count
- Historical: ~63-67% by area at w40
- The count vs area discrepancy explains most of the gap
