# REQUEST-OP movement handoff finding

**Verdict:** the missing eight-brigade delivery is a REQUEST selector/consumer contract mismatch, not a missing movement owner. At staged T16, 8 of the 10 selected 4th Corps brigades had fewer than the canonical `MIN_ATTACK_PERSONNEL = 500`; only the 441st (1,528) and 443rd (1,383) met the offensive floor.

`planDirectiveOperation` builds its reachable legal pool from availability, state-wide non-commitment, active operation-formation kind/status, objective eligibility, and friendly connected-component reachability. It does not apply `MIN_ATTACK_PERSONNEL`. The brigade dispatcher then calls `evaluateGarrisonAndDetachments` before `evaluateSectorAttack`. For the 442nd, retained T16 state is active brigade, 491 personnel, undisrupted, at Kružanj on its assigned sector front. Therefore its first rejecting predicate is exactly `491 < 500`: the garrison/detachment handler emits defend and returns, so operation approach routing is never reached. At T17 it had 488 personnel and neither a movement order nor movement state; none appears through T39. The same T16 floor failure applies to the 444th, 445th, 447th, 448th, 449th, 450th, and 4th Muslim brigades.

The comparison cases show the handoff owner exists. The 441st was above the floor, received a normal `bot_discretionary` column order to Trnovo at T17, followed a retained seven-node path during T18–T22, and attacked Tošići at T23. The 443rd was above the floor and already at Trnovo; it attacked Tošići at T21 and T22. Here `bot_discretionary` is the aggregator's label for bot-produced orders, while operation approach/staging authorizes the movement. Off-sector admission is therefore not itself defective: the reachable 441st was routed normally. No enclave, supply, donor, commitment, garrison, or sector guard is needed to explain the eight non-movers; the personnel floor precedes their operation-routing opportunity.

The retained Bukvik REQUEST proof is a useful non-battle-specific comparator: its 11-member roster was entirely above the floor (minimum 536), after which the same production owner recorded seven movements and nine operation-owned attackers. Separately, the authored historical-operation selector already rejects `below_min_attack_personnel`, disruption, and ordinary in-transit formations before roster admission.

**Smallest mechanical candidate:** apply the existing canonical 500-person offensive eligibility floor inside `planDirectiveOperation` before reachable-count/minimum-three selection. On this exact T16 state that leaves only two eligible brigades, so the ordinary query/consume contract should fail closed as `no_available_force` instead of admitting ten nominal participants of whom eight cannot be routed or attack. Aligning disruption/transit gates is a related contract audit, but neither caused this retained failure. No threshold change, force flag, teleport, strategic loan, or timing adjustment is proposed.

Scope: source and retained-evidence diagnosis only; no engine, query, stage, advance, test, or source edit was performed.
