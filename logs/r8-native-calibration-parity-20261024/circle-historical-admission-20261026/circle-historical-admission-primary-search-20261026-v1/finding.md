# Operation Circle primary-source admission search

**Verdict: UNRESOLVED_NAMED_OPERATION_AND_AGGREGATE_CONTROL.** This bounded search found no evidence of a calendar, objective, or brigade-pool authoring contradiction. It also did not authenticate **“Operation Circle/Krug”** as the name of a July/August 1992 ARBiH operation, nor the complete Donje Žešće or Tošići aggregate as captured and continuously held through January 1993. No content amendment is evidence-backed.

## Documented fact

The production mapping is explicit: scenario start **6 April 1992**, seven days per turn, so **t16 = 27 July 1992**. The existing late-July placement is therefore chronologically compatible with the strongest official evidence found.

In sworn ICTY testimony, the chief of staff of ARBiH/TO **Tactical Group II** described its summer 1992 zone as Hadžići–Ilidža–Hrasnica–Trnovo–Kalinovik. Its Rogoj purpose was to cut the Sarajevo–Kalinovik–Foča road and open communication toward eastern Bosnia. He dated the simultaneous attack on **Trnovo and Rogoj Pass to 31 July 1992**; Rogoj was lost on 2 August, recaptured overnight 2–3 August, and then held until the 1993 fall of Trnovo/Rogoj/Igman. A separate Kunarac transcript says Army of BiH forces took over Trnovo on 31 July. These are witness accounts in the court record, not a judicial finding of every locality’s controller. [ICTY Kunarac transcript, 24 May 2000, pp. 4023–4034](https://www.icty.org/x/cases/kunarac/trans/en/000524it.htm); [20 March 2000, pp. 392–395](https://www.icty.org/x/cases/kunarac/trans/en/000320it.htm).

The Mladić Trial Judgment documents a **different, opposing VRS action**: on 7 July 1992 Foča Tactical Group commander Marko Kovač ordered specified VRS units to help break the siege of Goražde. Those units cannot establish an ARBiH brigade pool, and that action must not be conflated with the 31 July Trnovo/Rogoj attack. [Mladić Trial Judgment, vol. I, ¶181 and n.663, 22 November 2017, pp. 97–98](https://www.icty.org/x/cases/mladic/tjug/en/171122-1of5_1.pdf).

One ICTY witness said that before the 31 May attack on Trnovo, most Serb residents withdrew toward Treskavica and **Tošići**. This identifies Tošići as a contemporary locality/refuge direction only; it does not establish its controller after July or control of the authored 12-settlement aggregate. [Krajišnik transcript, 18 October 2004, p. 7057](https://www.icty.org/x/cases/krajisnik/trans/en/041018ED.htm).

## Inference and model allocation

The t16 timing plausibly represents the documented late-July Trnovo/Rogoj effort and BB1’s broader late-July Goražde-corridor offensive. The authored single operation name, its Foča/Trnovo axis grouping, brigade assignments, and OSID objectives remain **model allocations**, not facts authenticated by this search. Exact-name searches returned no responsive official record. Constituent searches found no official capture/hold evidence for the six Donje Žešće settlements or twelve Tošići settlements. Search-index silence is not proof of absence.

The smallest supported next action is to obtain the underlying **TG II combat order/war diary and the Kunarac Exhibit 23 operational map for 31 July–3 August 1992**, then compare the named localities with the OSID constituent polygons. Until then, retain the present history-admission status and make no historical content change.
