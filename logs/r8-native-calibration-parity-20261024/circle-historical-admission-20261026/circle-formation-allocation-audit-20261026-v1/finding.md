# Operation Circle formation-allocation audit

**Verdict: `UNRESOLVED_FORMATION_IDENTITY`; no allocation amendment is supported.** The current allocation is not demonstrated to be historically wrong. It is a geographically coherent **model allocation whose finer July 1992 OOB identity remains unverified**.

The historical record supports a government-side 31 July Trnovo/Rogoj action and Muhamed Nogo's non-exhaustive participants: his unit, a TO Trnovo staff unit, units from Igman, and “7th Rijeka.” Husein Alić is the TG II witness. The cited pages do not prove TG II's formal corps chain, a full roster, an operation named Circle/Krug, or Donje Žešće/Tošići aggregate capture. BB1 p.187 supports a broader late-July regional counteroffensive, not these named brigade assignments.

The authored definition hosts Circle in modeled ARBiH 1st Corps and lists five Goražde formations (801st, 802nd, 808th, 843rd, 851st) plus the Hadžići-origin 102nd and 109th. Repository provenance sources every identity/command mapping to BB1 Appendix H's **October 1995** skeleton OOB and explicitly says personnel and timing are gameplay-authored. The Appendix places the Goražde formations under late-war 81st Independent; the game maps them to 1st Corps. Thus 1st Corps is explainable as the existing game host, not established July 1992 command truth.

At retained turn 15, all seven authored formations are active: the five Goražde units remain within the pocket; 102nd is at Tarčin and 109th at Kijevo. At t16 (27 July), Circle actually selects 801st, 802nd, 843rd, 851st, and 109th. The active 808th and 102nd are omitted by ordinary assembly/allocation. This runtime footprint responsibly approximates local Goražde forces plus an Igman/Trnovo-side element, but no source equates any one brigade with Nogo's unit, TO Trnovo staff unit, Igman units, or 7th Rijeka.

The modeled 4th Corps pool is not a sourced correction. At t15/t16, 443rd is at modeled Trnovo; 441st is near Mostar, 450th near Konjic, and 446th is absent at t16. Their identities also derive only from the October 1995 Appendix H. Neither later availability nor current personnel proves July 1992 participation. Do not divert them.

If a provisional model-inference pool must be stated, its defensible geographic boundary is the existing Goražde-local set `{801, 802, 808, 843, 851}` plus Hadžići/Igman-side `{102, 109}`. This is not an historical roster and guarantees no capture. There is no evidenced named equivalent for TO Trnovo or 7th Rijeka.

An ordinary data-only roster correction would require historian/canon review but does not by itself trigger the FORAWWV bright-line panel. Any amendment to Goražde enclave mechanics or sensitive historical representation invokes the applicable historian/gameplay review under `SENSITIVE_HISTORY_DESIGN_GATE.md`; a FORAWWV amendment or values bright-line crossing requires its mandated panel.
