# Cited wartime-artifact retrieval

**Result: neither requested wartime artifact was publicly retrieved or assigned a usable registry document ID.** Four official-domain searches returned the already-known Kunarac transcripts, not an order, war diary, or exhibit scan. The IRMCT Unified Court Records public landing page requires sign-in/registration to view records; no login, registration, or request was attempted.

## Artifact inventory

1. **Trial Exhibit 23 — identified, image unavailable.** The 20 March 2000 transcript records the Prosecution tendering Exhibits 1–36. At pp. 360–363, its research officer describes Exhibit 23 as a **1:50,000 trial map** of the municipalities relevant to the case, with areas circled in green. It depicts the main Sarajevo–Trnovo–Rogoj–Dobro Polje–Miljevina–Foča road and positions Goražde and Kalinovik. On 24 May, pp. 4023–4025, Husein Alić used this same map to indicate Tactical Group II's zone and discuss Rogoj. The transcript supplies no fuller exhibit/document identifier, source date, dated control overlay, or unit markings. The exhibit scan is absent from the unauthenticated selected-documents page. This is a courtroom orientation map, not an identified wartime operational map. [20 March transcript](https://www.icty.org/x/cases/kunarac/trans/en/000320it.htm); [24 May transcript](https://www.icty.org/x/cases/kunarac/trans/en/000524it.htm); [UCR selected documents](https://ucr.irmct.org/scasedocs/case/IT-96-23).

2. **TG II order/war diary — unidentified.** Neither transcript names or numbers a contemporaneous TG II order or war diary for 31 July–3 August 1992. The closest numbered artifact is **Defence Exhibit 69**, Husein Alić's later witness statement based on interviews of 3 June and 23 November 1999, admitted on 24 May 2000 (pp. 4036–4038). It is not a wartime record. Its transcript quotation dates fighting and the Rogoj control changes but does not expose an archival order ID.

3. **Unit evidence remains testimony.** Muhamed Nogo testified that his unit, the TO Trnovo staff unit, units from Igman, and “7th Rijeka” took part at Rogoj (pp. 3978–3979). This is not an order of battle and does not establish TG II's formal chain, complete participants, routes, or the authored brigade pool.
