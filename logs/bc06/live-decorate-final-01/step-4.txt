STAFF RECOMMENDATION

Staff recommendation for this abstract command decision. This is not a historical default and does not control bot calibration.

You judge that with casualties this heavy, a decoration would ring hollow or look like spin. You hold the citations back. The deserving go unhonoured, and some read the silence as the leadership looking away from their sacrifice.

Republic of Bosnia and Herzegovina morale -1
Republic of Bosnia and Herzegovina Internal Cohesion -1
SITUATION

Your staff brings forward citations for units that have held the line under impossible conditions. A decoration — a unit citation, a streamer for the colours — costs nothing in materiel and can lift a battered formation's spirit and the watching army's morale. But honours are political: the unit you single out is the unit you are seen to favour, and every formation passed over reads the omission. Decorate the wrong unit, or decorate at all amid heavy losses, and the gesture rings hollow or breeds resentment. Which formation will the president honour — and only a regular formation of the armed forces is eligible?

CORPS COMMANDER NEDŽAD AJNADŽIĆ

Staff notes that decorations honouring regular formations can lift morale but are double-edged: the favoured unit gains, the overlooked resent, and honours amid heavy loss can ring hollow. Declining is the conservative course.

TRIGGER EVIDENCE
Recurring command-presence pressure is active after prolonged front-line strain.
Eligibility is restricted to regular armed-forces formations; no irregular or paramilitary unit is offered.
