# Operation Circle delivery diagnosis

**Finding:** Circle did not fail because the operation was rejected or because its selected brigades failed to arrive. It was admitted at t16 (27 July 1992) with five of seven authored brigades, entered execution, and made seven attacks. The failure mechanism is an under-concentrated admitted roster: both missing targets were assigned one brigade because the second authored brigade on each axis was rejected at injection by Circle’s ordinary assembly-reachability rule.

At retained post-turn t15 all seven were active. Circle has `planning_duration: 4`; injection therefore permits a non-forward-deployed formation only when its exact staging OSID is reachable through friendly control within `planning_duration - 1 = 3` hops. The 808th at `gorazde:slatina_2` needed four friendly hops to Foča-axis staging `gorazde:faocici_2`; the 102nd at `hadzici:tarcin_2` needed six to Trnovo-axis staging `trnovo:delijas`. Both deterministically fail `canReachAxisStaging` as `staging_unreachable`. The 843rd needed two hops and was selected; the 109th was already in the Trnovo objective municipality, so the forward-deployed predicate admitted it. The three Goražde-perimeter brigades were likewise local and selected.

No axis-level assembly floor then required the omitted partner, no operation-level `minimum_assembled_participants` was authored, and the effective viable floor was only two for the whole operation. By retained post-turn t16 the stored roster was exactly `{801,802,843,851,109}`. The 843rd received a `bot_discretionary` order to Podgrab rather than exact authored staging; this was still lawful delivery because Podgrab is adjacent to Donje Žešće. The 109th remained at Kijevo, adjacent to Tošići. Both therefore owned and executed real attacks through the ordinary resolver.

Results were material defeat, not silent rejection: 843rd attacked Donje Žešće at t18/t19/t20 with ratios 0.60/0.93/0.61 (repulsed/stalemate/repulsed); 109th attacked Tošići at t18 at 0.46 (catastrophic). Circle ended t24 `max_failures`, capturing only its three Goražde objectives; both failed objectives remain RS in post-turn saves 33, 34 and 39. The corrected candidate versus old parent kept the same t16 roster, axes, three captures, two failures and t24 terminal; it added one Foča retry (7 vs 6 total attacks) but did not alter delivery topology.

**ACTUAL vs UNREPORTED:** the retained saves, graph, temporal log, AAR and battles establish the admission exclusions, physical attack positions and combat outcomes. The default run did not enable `preplanned_eligibility`, `axis_reject`, or `route_decision`; therefore the phase-exact t16 rejection receipts, t17 opening-predictor ratios, and the precise t16 rule issuing 843rd’s Podgrab order are **UNREPORTED**, not favorable. If required, the smallest replay is save_w15→completed t18 with only those three topics, preserving pre/post formations, movement owners, operation roster, gate facts, attack orders and battles; stop at t18 or first fidelity/hash failure. No campaign is warranted.

**Smallest calibration lever for independent review:** change only Circle’s existing generic `planning_duration` assembly budget from 4 to 7. Seven covers the observed six-hop 102nd and four-hop 808th routes under the existing `duration - 1` admission rule, without changing t16 availability, authored pool, targets, thresholds, control ownership or baseline. It is an assembly-budget proposal, not a success claim; no implementation should precede independent review.
