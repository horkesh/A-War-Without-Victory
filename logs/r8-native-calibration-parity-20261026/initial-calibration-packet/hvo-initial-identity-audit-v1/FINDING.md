# HVO April-1992 identity and availability audit

**Verdict: the sources do not support these ten rows as ten distinct active brigades at their candidate turn-1/2 placements. One three-row lineage duplication is source-proven; the other seven require identity/date correction or remain unresolved.** This is not authority to delete valid local forces.

The runtime chain is direct: scenario `player_choice` + `init_formations_oob` loads `oob_brigades.json`; every audited row says `available_from:0`. Nine mandatory rows are active in `initial_save` at their authored home OSIDs. Elective `hvo_nikola_subic_zrinski_brigade` is absent initially, then recruitment creates it at turn 1 at fallback `op:busovaca:busovaca_2` (not its authored `buselji_2`) with `activation_turn:7`; exact-ID/oob-tag matching does not canonicalize aliases. No lifecycle merge/rename covers any audited ID.

**Source-proven conflict:** BB2 p.446 says the Ban Josip Jelačić Brigade was established **20 December 1992** from Kiseljak, Kreševo and Fojnica municipal HVO headquarters, and later split into the 94th “Ban Josip Jelačić” and 95th “Marinko Bošnjak” regiments; BB2 p.460 repeats that lineage. The candidate instead fields `hrhb_ban_jelai_brigade`, `hrhb_94th_brigade`, and `hrhb_95th_brigade` separately from April. They are not three April formations. `hrhb_kiseljak_brigade` and `hrhb_kreevo_brigade` may abstract the precursor municipal forces, but the sources do not establish them as distinct 700-person active brigades at Brnjaci/Polje; status is **UNRESOLVED**, not duplicate-by-assumption.

**Other identities:** BB2 p.437 distinguishes the territorial Vitez brigade (~1,500) from the company-sized PPN Vitezovi formed late 1992/early 1993. The single elite/motorized `hrhb_vitezovi_brigade_vitez` conflates them, while the April scenario master says “Vitezovi Brigade forming”; exact active turn-0 identity is unsupported. BB1 p.520 is a later OOB, not April proof: it lists Nikola Šubić Zrinski/93rd, Stjepan Tomašević/90th, Travnik/91st, and Jure Francetić as a battalion. The lower-tier repo cross-reference dates Nikola (20 Dec 1992), Jure (15 Dec 1992), and Stjepan (5 Jul 1992), but those dates need authoritative-source confirmation before mutation. `hrhb_travnik_brigade` also lacks an April formation/date/location citation. Thus `hrhb_jure_franceti_brigade`, `hrhb_stjepan_tomaevi_brigade`, `hrhb_travnik_brigade`, and `hvo_nikola_subic_zrinski_brigade` are **April unsupported / exact date unresolved**.

**Smallest exact supported change:** `hrhb_ban_jelai_brigade.available_from` cannot remain `0`; BB's 20 December date corresponds to about turn 36. Do not implement that alone: first resolve whether the runtime should carry the precursor municipal forces, the Ban brigade, and later 94th/95th identities as timed rename/split phases. Otherwise a one-row delay leaves the same lineage duplicated.
