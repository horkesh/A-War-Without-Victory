The successful Bihać ops (Tigar-Sloboda 4/4, APWB Pressure 5/5) captured 9 objectives, but these are in the **KRAJINA region where painted=RBiH-majority** — flipping them produces "match→match" (no net delta vs painted) or "RS→RBiH" (closer to painted). Yet net RBiH OSID count dropped.

This implies **ARBiH lost more OSIDs to VRS counter-attacks during 188w than the 9 it captured in Bihać**. The most likely losers are CENTRAL_BOSNIA OSIDs (ARBiH 89 sim vs 110 painted — biggest deficit) and HERZEGOVINA (8 sim vs 4 painted, +4 over-extended — but inverse direction).

**Where ARBiH LOST ground:** harder to localize without n1955 painted-compare in hand, but the −4 OSID net delta combined with +5,963 RS personnel attrition and +6,936 RS KIA suggests **VRS counter-attacked elsewhere and won OSIDs back**. ## §7. Top 3 next-fix candidates (impact-per-LOC)

Ordered by ratio of likely territorial / calibration impact to engineering effort.

### #1 — Fix the paper-flip behavior in ARBiH Bihać successes (HIGH impact / LOW LOC)

**Observation:** Tigar-Sloboda and APWB Pressure captured 9 objectives with `total_attacks=0` and zero casualties on both sides. The objectives flipped without combat resolution. This is the same "zero-eligible-attacker" anomaly flagged in calibration memory (n1281 noted 157/396 = 39% ZEA ops).

**Hypothesis:** Once `commander_confidence` cleared and the catalog approved, the op-launch path is finding the objectives' defender power below threshold and flipping them on the objective-state evaluation pass rather than running attack resolution. Either (a) defenders fled / dispersed mid-war, leaving these OSIDs effectively un-defended at the time the op fires, or (b) the launch path short-circuits when defender_power < threshold.

**Fix candidate:** In the op-resolution pipeline, require `total_attacks ≥ 1` for an op to be marked `outcome: success`. If the op launches with zero eligible attackers OR finds zero defenders, mark it `paper_flip` or `aborted_no_contact` and do NOT credit it as a success. This separates "successful operations" from "objectives that fell because nobody was home" in the AAR ledger and prevents the paper-flip from contaminating ARBiH commander_state confidence updates.

**Why high impact:** Restores the integrity of the "did ARBiH actually accomplish anything" signal for downstream consumers (commander_confidence, theater_assessment, political_legitimacy events). Currently 2 of 2 ARBiH "successes" are paper, while the 6 genuine attempts all failed with heavy casualties. The success/failure ratio that commanders read is misleading.

**Estimated LOC:** ~20-40 lines in `src/sim/combat/sector_offensive.ts` (op outcome classifier) or wherever `outcome: success` is assigned. The Wave 2 forensics already pinpoints the resolution path.

### #2 — Unblock the 5 remaining ARBiH opportunities (kupres_cincar_94, sana_95, sana_95_follow_on, mistral_2_95, vlasic_ridge_95) (HIGH impact / MEDIUM LOC)
