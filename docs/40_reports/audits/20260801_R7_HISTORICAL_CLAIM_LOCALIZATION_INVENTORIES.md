The sixth made spans clause-local but recognized anaphoric continuation only when `proceed|continue` immediately preceded `anyway|regardless`, and it ignored an accountability purpose in that clause. The seventh recognized reordered markers and the first purpose, but an early review sanitized later operative actions and `operation\w*` misread adjectival `operational`. The eighth assigned purpose to a finite list of recognized actions; unseen operative verbs and review phrases containing an operative noun still escaped. The ninth classified exact purpose nouns but still tied the antecedent to the immediately preceding hard clause, inspected only the first continuation, and let a negator elsewhere in the clause suppress a later continuation. The tenth introduced the forward state machine but still cut a leading `Regardless,` away from its continuation, failed to carry a local negation across a coordinated continuation list, and recognized only a narrow warning-negation word order. This eleventh correction completes those structural relations without adding phrase-specific production exceptions. It preserves production triggers, narratives, sources, essays, mechanics, save schema, the existing four-artifact baseline refresh, package/version/release state, and `FORAWWV`.

Post-review found one residual predicate-scope error: hyphenated `no-one` established a false warning antecedent, while do-support negation followed by `only|merely|simply` erased a warning that the focus construction asserts occurred. This twelfth correction parses that relation structurally. A focus limiter after `do|does|did ... not` or its straight/curly-apostrophe contraction makes the warning operative; the same limiter before the negated auxiliary remains a genuine warning negation. Negative subjects accept whitespace or a bounded hyphen class and remain case-insensitive. Comma and hard-clause ownership prevent a limiter from leaking into another subclause. The forward continuation state machine, purpose and accountability rules, exact paramilitary exception, and every production/content contract above are unchanged.

Independent review found that the twelfth correction still decided warning truth with a finite focus-limiter list, so unlisted post-negation modifiers such as `credibly`, `clearly`, `adequately`, `explicitly`, `directly`, `solely`, and `exclusively` incorrectly erased an operative warning. This thirteenth correction replaces that denylist with a positive categorical grammar: only an exact negative form immediately adjoining the warning predicate suppresses the antecedent. Any token between `not|n't` and `warn` fails closed, while `probably|apparently` around do-support or a contraction explicitly preserves the warning. `ever|once` remain conservatively operative rather than receiving a special safe allowance. The broader local-negation grammar remains owned only by continuation actions, and the continuation state machine, purpose/accountability rules, exact paramilitary exception, and production/content contracts are unchanged.

Final structural review found that the thirteenth correction still overclaimed completeness in two directions. Its categorical regex retained an arbitrary internal `-ly` slot and could restart at a contraction, `never`, refusal, or negative subject after an unseen epistemic modifier; it also received only the warning predicate's start offset, so qualification after `warn|caution|object|oppose|reject` was invisible. This fourteenth correction removes the arbitrary slot, retains only the defended positive safe modifiers `simply|merely|emphatically`, and inspects the categorical match boundary for the generic structural class `-ly|maybe|perhaps|almost`. The full warning span now owns a bridge to the first sensitive object in the same comma subclause: only whitespace plus the corpus-defended complement tokens `about|of|the|to` may suppress the antecedent; any other word or punctuation fails closed. No epistemic or post-predicate modifier denylist was added. Conservative false positives remain preferable to erasing a warning antecedent, and every continuation/action/content contract remains unchanged.
