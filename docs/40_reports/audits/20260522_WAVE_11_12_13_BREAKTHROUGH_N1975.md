## TL;DR

n1975 is the first material territorial movement since n1968 (Wave 8). RS lost 27 OSIDs net (5 below painted floor), HRHB gained 13, RBiH gained 14.

The breakthrough is **not** primarily an effect of Wave 11/12/13 unblocking *new* op captures. Of 35 late-war (turn >=150) flips, **only 2** are attributed to the formal operation flow (Operation Vlasic Ridge `op:travnik:varosluk`, Operation Cincar/Kupres `op:kupres:bucovaca`). The other 33 are `control_events` of `mechanism=combat` driven by **brigades that participated in earlier operations** but are now flipping OSIDs *outside* any active op (or after their op ended). The flips concentrate in **East Herzegovina** (Trebinje-Bileca-Ljubinje-Gacko-Foca, 13 OSIDs, mostly by `arbih_4th_corps:Operacija Celik` brigades and HVO Jackal/Grude brigades) and **Donji Vakuf** (6 OSIDs by `arbih_727th_slavna`, an `arbih_3rd_corps` Operation Vlasic Ridge brigade).

The two highlighted anomalies have distinct root causes:

- **Mistral 1 fr=0**: Op authored with `faction=""` (blank), brigades attached week 1 but immediately stripped (week 2 brigade_count=0). `total_attacks=0`, `participating_brigades=[]` in the final record. Wave 13's launch-floor patch is *unrelated* — this op never reached the launch gate. It died at corps-assignment / faction-resolution before brigades stayed attached.

- **Jajce Recovery no_approach_osid (Wave 11+12)**: 8 of 11 targets (all Jajce-mun OSIDs plus `mrkonjic_grad:podrasnica_2`) are **NOT in any `arbih_3rd_corps` sub-segment's `enemy_osids`** — they lie *beyond the front line*. Wave 11's helper-fallback only restores approach OSIDs from sub-segments the corps actually fronts; the patch is functioning correctly. The data shape is the issue: Wave 12 re-corps'd Jajce 95 to `arbih_3rd_corps` but did **not** extend any `arbih_3rd_corps:1` sub-segment to cover the full Jajce ring.

## Q4: Mistral 1 force_ratio=0 root cause

Full record from `operation_history`:

```

capture_provenance:     "no_objectives_held"
axis_summaries:
  - mistral_1_grahovo: 4 targets (Bosansko Grahovo), brigades=[]
  - mistral_1_glamoc:  4 targets (Glamoc),           brigades=[]
weekly_log[0] (turn 160, planning):  brigade_count=4, attacks=0
weekly_log[1] (turn 161, recovery):  brigade_count=0, attacks=0
```

**Root cause**: Mistral 1 reached its launch turn (t160) with 4 brigades attached (week 0 `brigade_count=4`) but transitioned to `phase="recovery"` on week 1 with `brigade_count=0`. The brigades were stripped before the launch gate could evaluate force ratio. Because no axis ever fielded brigades, the force_ratio estimator divides by 0 attacker_power -> **fr=0**. The `defender_power_too_high` recovery reason is the standard label the abort path emits when fr < launch_floor (0 < any floor).

**Wave 13 is unrelated**: Wave 13 changes the launch *floor* when `op.min_attack_outcome=='repulsed'`. Mistral 1 never reached the launch-floor comparison with a finite fr — the brigades disappeared from the op before the comparison was meaningful. In n1974, Mistral 1 likely never *finalized* (or aborted at a different phase), which is why the fr was non-zero (>0.x) — n1975's earlier brigade-stripping merely surfaces a divide-by-zero edge case in the fr estimator.

**The `faction=""` field is the deeper smell**: `hvo_main_staff` is the corps_id but the op record lost the `faction` string. This indicates the op was authored by the HVO main-staff system (cross-corps, multi-axis) without going through a per-corps `faction` resolver. When brigades from sub-corps (likely `hvo_tomislavgrad` and `hvo_northwest_bosnia`) were claimed for Mistral 1, the lack of a faction guard let the corps re-allocator drain them after one turn.

**Two distinct bugs at play**:
1. Mistral 1 author writes `faction=""` instead of `"HRHB"` (data/catalog issue in HVO main-staff op definition).
2. Mistral 1's brigades are released by their *own* corps before the op can use them (cross-corps op brigade-protection missing).

### Why `no_approach_osid` is *correct behavior*

Wave 11's `collectObjectiveApproachOsids` sub-segment fallback works as follows: when the corps-level approach scan returns empty, scan each sub-segment's `enemy_osids` and pick approach OSIDs **adjacent to the targets**. The fallback functions correctly — but the 8 deep-Jajce targets have no adjacency through any 3rd-corps sub-segment. The helper has nothing to return.

The axis_summary records `blocker = "no_approach_osid"` because the *aggregate* axis (which covers all 11 targets) cannot satisfy approach-OSID derivation for the majority. The op enters recovery without launching — `total_attacks = 0`, no strength bleed.

### Why Wave 12 didn't help

Wave 12 added bridge OSIDs to the `federation_western_bosnia` catalog and re-corps'd Jajce 95 to `arbih_3rd_corps`. But Wave 12 changed the **catalog / op authoring layer**, not the **front-sector topology layer**. The bridge OSIDs only matter if a sector sub-segment exposes them as `enemy_osids` or `friendly_osids`. The `arbih_3rd_corps:1` sub-segment (the only one with any Jajce-area enemy_osids) covers Donji Vakuf rear + 1 Jajce OSID (grdovo) + Sipovo/Skender Vakuf bridges. The rest of the Jajce ring is still firmly inside `vrs_1st_krajina` territory with no shared 3rd-corps front edge.

1. **Sub-segment extension via Wave-12 bridge OSIDs**: Wave 12 added bridge OSIDs intended to draw `arbih_3rd_corps:1` deeper into Jajce. The sector consolidation pipeline did not pick them up as enemy_osids. Hand off to **sector-expert** to verify whether the bridge OSIDs should appear in the `enemy_osids` set of `arbih_3rd_corps:1` after sector recomputation.
