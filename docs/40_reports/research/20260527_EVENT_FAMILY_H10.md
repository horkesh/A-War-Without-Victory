## 1. Cited Historical Narrative

The Washington Agreement of **18 March 1994** committed HRHB and RBiH to constituting a **joint Federation Army** from HVO and ARBiH units. The Vienna Framework Agreement of **26 March 1994** set a command-integration timetable. The historical record is unambiguous that **formal commitment was made but operational integration was slow, partial, and uneven** throughout 1994 and into 1995.

Documented in:

- **Washington Agreement text** Articles V-VI — joint Federation Army to be constituted from HVO and ARBiH; unified command structure to be established; integrated officer corps; common rank structure; unified procurement.
- **Vienna Framework Agreement** (26 March 1994) — phased command integration: (Phase 1) cease-fire and disengagement; (Phase 2) joint command headquarters; (Phase 3) integrated operational units; (Phase 4) fully unified Federation Army. The timetable was aspirational and was not met.
- **Split Agreement** (22 July 1995) between Tuđman (Croatia), Izetbegović (RBiH), and Zubak (HRHB) — formalized **mutual defense cooperation** including HV expeditionary support, joint operational planning for the Bihać relief axis, and codified the operational-coordination framework that had developed ad hoc since Washington. The Split Agreement is the operational watershed: it acknowledged that full institutional integration had not happened, and substituted **operational coordination** as the working model.
- **Prlić et al. IT-04-74-T Trial Judgment** Vol. 3 §§201-340 documents:
  - HVO command chain remained operationally distinct from ARBiH command chain throughout 1994-1995 (separate Main Staff, separate logistics, separate officer corps).
  - HR HB institutional organs (Presidency, Government, Defense Department) continued meeting in Mostar through 1994 and into 1995.
  - **Tuđman Presidential Transcripts** of April 1994 - July 1995 record Tuđman directing Zubak to preserve HVO institutional autonomy inside the Federation framework, treat Federation Army integration as a phased process under Zagreb control, and use the Federation as a **diplomatic instrument** rather than as a true unification.
  - **Tuđman quote (Prlić Trial Judgment Vol. 3 §218, paraphrasing the transcript)**: the Federation was to be treated as "an arrangement that gives us breathing space and international standing, not an arrangement that abolishes Herzeg-Bosna."
- **Balkan Battlegrounds Vol. II pp. 453-461** describes the Federation Army integration as **on paper rapid, in practice slow**: HVO and ARBiH continued to operate as separate forces in 1994; joint operations were *coordinated*, not *unified*; the first genuinely joint operation at force level (as opposed to coordinated parallel operations) was the Bihać relief axis in late summer 1995. BB notes that the late-1995 joint operations (Maestral, Maestral 2, Sana, Mistral) showed real operational integration that the early-1994 framework had not produced.

The **historical actor** — the HR HB Presidency, the HVO Main Staff, and Zagreb (Tuđman) — pursued **slow, partial, foot-dragging institutional integration combined with effective operational coordination from mid-1995 onward**. This is the documented historical posture, not a counterfactual.

## 2. Defensible Historical/Default Option

H10 is authored as a **follow-on decision** on the integration tempo, gated by H9 outcome. Per packet §4 H10 row, the counterfactual column is `n/a` — H10 does not introduce a new player-decision counterfactual. Phase A worksheet recommends a **two-option** structure where both options describe **historically-attested postures** at different points on the 1994-1995 integration timeline:

- **Label:** `coordinate` — Accept the Federation framework formally; preserve HVO institutional autonomy; pursue operational coordination with ARBiH on a case-by-case basis; defer full command integration. **Historical-default** if H9 resolved `accept`.
- **Rationale:** This describes the documented historical posture of HRHB / HVO command throughout 1994 and the early part of 1995. The HVO Main Staff retained command autonomy; HR HB Presidency continued; operational coordination with ARBiH developed gradually. The eventual late-1995 joint operations (Maestral, Maestral 2, Sana, Mistral, Bihać relief) demonstrated that effective coordination did not require institutional unification. Per Prlić Vol. 3 §§201-340 and BB Vol. II pp. 453-461.

## 3. Proposed Counterfactual Options

### Option: `full_integration`

- **Label:** `full_integration` — Commit immediately to full command integration; subordinate HVO Main Staff to a unified Federation Army HQ; dissolve HR HB parallel institutions; treat the Federation as a true unification.
- **Historical analogy:** The Washington Agreement and Vienna Framework Agreement **on paper** committed to this outcome. The counterfactual models the Tuđman-Zubak axis choosing to implement the framework as written rather than as the foot-dragging reality. Historical record: Filipović and Divjak both recorded (in corroborated memoir testimony admitted at Prlić as background) that ARBiH officers expected genuine integration in early 1994 and were repeatedly disappointed; Federation Army integration was an aspiration that the HVO command-and-political base did not share.
- **Design provenance:** `design_counterfactual` — no faction document at the HR HB Presidency or HV General Staff level adopted this posture in 1994-1995. The branch counterfactually models the Tuđman tape-transcripts' "breathing space" framing being reversed: Federation as genuine unification rather than diplomatic instrument.
- **Sensitive-history check:** Confirmed — `full_integration` authorizes no atrocity. It is a command-structural decision. By accelerating institutional unification, it foreshortens HR HB parallel-institution continuity (a Prlić-documented JCE continuation surface), which is **canonically appropriate** — counterfactually closing the structural conditions that the Prlić Trial Chamber found enabled the 1993-1994 atrocities (Vol. 4 §§44-67 on JCE).
- **Cost floor (Phase D required):** `patron_confidence: -15` (Zagreb actively opposed full integration per Tuđman transcripts), `internal_cohesion: -10` HRHB (HVO political base and HR HB institutional cadre lose autonomy), `alliance_change: +0.2` (RBiH reads this as good-faith partner behavior). Phase D scenario testing must verify `full_integration` does not trivially dominate `coordinate` — the patron and cohesion costs should make `coordinate` the dominant choice under historical-bot calibration.

### Option: `parallel_institutions`

- **Label:** `parallel_institutions` — Reject the integration tempo even nominally; preserve HR HB as a fully separate entity inside the Federation paper-framework; treat the Federation as exclusively a defense pact against the VRS with no internal-institutional integration.
- **Historical analogy:** The historical posture was foot-dragging within nominal compliance. `parallel_institutions` counterfactually goes further: explicit refusal of even token integration, preservation of HR HB as a fully separate state-within-a-state. Approximated in operational fact by the HR HB Presidency's continued meetings, Mostar's continued role as HR HB capital, and the persistence of separate HVO logistics through 1994-1995 — but the historical posture stopped short of *publicly* refusing the integration framework. This branch models that refusal becoming explicit.
- **Design provenance:** `design_counterfactual` — the historical record shows institutional drag, not public refusal. The branch counterfactually models the HR HB Presidency choosing to author the refusal publicly.
- **Sensitive-history check:** Confirmed — `parallel_institutions` authorizes no atrocity. The structural ambiguity that contributed to the Prlić JCE findings is **preserved** in this branch, which means downstream H13 (third-entity counterfactual) becomes more reachable. Per packet §3.6, this is canonically acceptable: the branch does not author atrocity, it authors institutional separation. Downstream Ring 1/2 consequences (if any) emerge from mechanical conditions (alliance instability, RBiH-HRHB friction re-escalation), not from author intent.
- **Cost floor (Phase D required):** `patron_confidence: +10` (Zagreb explicitly approves preserved HR HB autonomy per Tuđman transcripts), `international_standing: -15` (Washington reads the public refusal as bad faith), `alliance_change: -0.3` (RBiH reads as defection from Federation framework), `equipment_quality_modifier` ceiling reduction on HV expeditionary support pipeline (Zagreb still backs HRHB, but U.S. pressure on Zagreb caps the support).
