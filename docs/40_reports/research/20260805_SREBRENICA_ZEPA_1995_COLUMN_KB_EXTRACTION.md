## HEADLINE FINDING (read this first)

**The KB does NOT contain the main narrative of the July 1995 fall of Srebrenica or the 28th Division column.**

The BB1 index (`BB1_p0535`) places the fall narrative — including the "Krivaja 95" entry (BB1 print p.322) and "Orić 317-325" — at **BB1 print pages ~317-325**. With the KB's consistent file-to-print offset of ~37 (e.g. `BB1_p0074` = print p.38; `BB1_p0401` = print p.364; `BB1_p0432` = print p.395), those narrative pages are **files `BB1_p0353`-`BB1_p0362`, which are ALL MISSING from the extracted KB.** The extracted BB1 jumps from the mid-book straight to `BB1_p0401` (= print p.364, the western-Bosnia/Bihać/Deliberate Force endgame).

What survives in the KB about the 1995 fall is therefore only: (a) the **chronology** table, (b) a **retrospective analytical paragraph**, (c) the **Section VII endnotes** for the missing chapter, and (d) the post-fall **reconstituted-OOB** annex entry. This confirms and extends the prior historian pass: the substantive facts are concentrated in the **endnotes**, and the detailed column narrative is **absent**.

## 2. Fall of Srebrenica, July 1995 — citable facts

**F2.1** VRS operation to seize the enclave was code-named **"Krivaja 95."** — *BB1 p.443 en.274; BB1 index p.322 ("Krivaja 95" 322).*

**F2.2** The **RS Supreme Command strategic directive for 1995 (dated 8 March 1995)** called for the VRS "**to strangle the Srebrenica and Zepa enclaves and seize them if the UN withdrew**." — *BB1 p.435 en.17; cross-ref BB1 p.434 en.16.* (This is the "Directive 7" strangle order; BB frames it as strangle-then-seize.)

**F2.3** **VRS conducted preliminary actions around Srebrenica in early June 1995**; the attack "appears to have been delayed by the ARBiH Sarajevo offensive"; **the ARBiH raid on 28 June against a Serb village just outside the enclave then sparked the VRS operation.** — *BB1 p.435 en.17.*

**F2.4** **President Karadžić approved the operation to seize the enclave** — established from VRS documents (incl. the 8 March directive) produced at the **Krstić trial**, ICTY OTP Exhibits 425 and 426. — *BB1 p.434 en.16.*

**F2.5** BB assesses "**it seems unlikely that Milošević knew of or approved the mass VRS executions**... There is **no evidence that Serbian RDB, MUP or VJ personnel took part in these actions**" (i.e. in the executions, as distinct from the 1993 campaign where VJ/MUP involvement was documented). — *BB1 p.435 en.17.*

**F2.6 VRS command of Krivaja 95:** the **1st Bratunac Brigade HQ under Lt. Col. Vidoje Blagojević** and a **forward HQ of the 1st Zvornik Brigade under Lt. Col. Vinko Pandurević** controlled troops in their sectors and "probably reported directly to **Drina Corps** headquarters." The role of TG "Pribićevac" HQ is unclear. — *BB1 p.443 en.274.* (BB also identifies **Radislav Krstić** as the Drina Corps figure tried at ICTY — BB1 p.434 en.16.)

**F2.7** BB explicitly states its information on "VRS organization, planning, and actions for and during 'Krivaja 95' — **including the battles against the 28th Division column after the enclave's fall**" — "has been derived largely from... **the transcripts of General Krstić's trial at the ICTY**," specifically testimony of **27, 28, 29, 30 June 2000.** — *BB1 p.443 en.274.* (Signals BB's own column facts live in the missing narrative and are ICTY-sourced.)

**F2.8 Chronology (11-12 July 1995):** "**Bosnian Serb troops break through Dutch UN defenses and overrun Srebrenica safe area, forcing thousands of Muslims to flee north to a UN base at Potočari**" (11 July); 12 July UNSC demands VRS withdrawal, NATO demands release of Dutch peacekeepers held by the VRS. — *BB1 p.074 (chronology).*

**F2.9** Prewar/enclave population figures (from endnotes, secondary): **36,666** residents (Honig & Both) or **37,211** (Rohde); Rohde separately gives "**a prewar population of about 9,000**." — *BB1 p.443 en.246-249.*

**F2.10** BB notes Serb expulsion of civilians from the seized enclave and mass-grave/execution reporting, but **only via press-article endnote citations** (Washington Post, NYT, CSM, Oct 1995-Apr 1996), NOT via extracted narrative or an aggregate death toll. — *BB1 p.446 en.386-424.*

## 3. The 28th Division

**F3.1** BB refers to "the **ARBiH 28th Division**" as the Srebrenica-enclave formation destroyed/dispersed in July 1995. — *BB1 p.443 en.268; p.446 en.427.*

**F3.2 Reconstitution (post-fall OOB):** a **28th Independent Division, HQ Živinice**, was **"formed from a merger of the 24th Division, HQ Živinice, and the remnants of the Srebrenica-based 28th Division in July-August 1995,"** then **subordinated directly to the ARBiH General Staff** (detached from 2nd Corps). — *BB1 p.514.* Listed component brigades of this reconstituted division: 210th Vitezka Liberation "Nesib Malkić" (HQ Živinice), 240th Muslim Mountain, 241st Spreča Muslim Light "Gazije" (HQ Kalesija), 242nd **Zvornik** Muslim Light (HQ Kalesija), 243rd Muslim-Podrinje Mountain (HQ Kladanj), 244th (Kladanj), 245th (Kalesija), 246th Vitezka Mountain (HQ **Sapna**), 286th (Stupari), 287th (Vitalj), 24th Sabotage Bn "Black Wolves," 24th Antiterrorist Co "Živinice Wasps." — *BB1 p.514.*

**F3.3 Commander:** **NOT CONFIRMABLE from the KB.** Naser Orić is indexed at BB1 pp.317-325 (the missing narrative pages) and pp.346, 352, 406; the KB contains **no extracted statement** that Orić had left the enclave before July 1995. Treat "Orić absent in 1995" as **NOT IN KB** (it is well-established in ICTY/secondary literature but is not citable here).

## 4. The column / breakout ("March of Death")

**NOT IN KB as narrative.** No extracted page describes the column's route, start point (Šušnjari/Buljim as a *1995* jump-off), day-by-day timeline, or losses en route.

**F4.1** **"Baljkovica" and "Nezuk" do not appear anywhere in the KB** (0 hits across all BB1+BB2 pages). The Baljkovica-corridor / Nezuk breakthrough is **not citable from this KB.**

**F4.2** The **existence** of the breakout to ARBiH lines is attested only obliquely, via endnote press-citations: "**Bosnian Soldiers Evade Serbs in Trudge to Safety**" (Washington Post, 18 July 1995) and "**Muslim Refugees Slip Across Serb Lines**" (NYT, 18 July 1995). — *BB1 p.446 en.386, 390, 425.* No figures for column strength or survivors are given in the extracted text.

**F4.3** "Buljim" appears once (`BB2_p0416`) but as a **1993** Rohde quote about fighting for the Buljim peak overlooking the enclave's NW corner — **not** the 1995 column start. — *BB2 p.416 (Annex 36 endnote, quoting Rohde, Endgame p.8).*

**F4.4** BB's own (missing) account of "the battles against the 28th Division column after the enclave's fall" is explicitly **ICTY-Krstić-sourced** (see F2.7) — i.e. even BB's column narrative is a secondary rendering of ICTY testimony, not independent.

## 5. Reconstitution figures (the key quantitative fact)

**F5.1** The sole citable instance: an ARBiH commander (Delić) statement of **August 1995 that 2,700 Srebrenica survivors had joined up with a newly reconstituted 28th Division after the enclave's fall**, together with "**official Bosnian Army estimates stating that some 3,200 ARBiH soldiers remain unaccounted for from the Srebrenica battles, massacres, and escape attempt.**" — *BB1 p.443 en.268* (BB cites *Sarajevo Prva Linija*, July 1997). **Exact wording confirmed.**

**F5.2** BB adds that surviving 28th Division members "**proved extremely reluctant to register themselves with any of the international organizations**," so UNHCR refugee tallies (35,632 registered in Tuzla-area muns as of 5 Aug 1994, a figure that also mixed in Žepa refugees) likely **omitted most surviving 28th Division soldiers.** — *BB1 p.446 en.427.*

**F5.3** The "2,700"/"3,200" strings appear on two pages, but **only `BB1_p0443` is the reconstitution figure**; the "2,700" on `BB1_p0441` is an unrelated Sarajevo-siege artillery endnote (Red Army 12,700 heavy guns, 2,000 rocket launchers). Do not double-cite p.441.

**Engine-model implication (data, not new fact):** the only KB-grounded split is **≈2,700 reconstituted (rejoined force) vs ≈3,200 unaccounted (killed/missing/executed/lost in escape)** out of the fighting-age male cohort. Any displacement/reconstitution ratio in the engine should trace to this single endnote or be flagged as a designer override.

## 6. Žepa, late July 1995

**F6.1** **Chronology:** "**Bosnian Serb forces launch a major attack on the UN-declared safe area of Žepa**" (14 July); "**press attack on Žepa with tanks and artillery, while Bosnian Government troops disarm UN Ukrainian troops, hoping to use them as hostages**" (18 July); "**Serb atrocities associated with their capture of Zepa and Srebrenica eventually bring worldwide condemnation.**" — *BB1 p.074.*

**F6.2** Endnote details: Žepa civilian estimates "had ranged as high as 16,000" but later "appeared... inaccurate or inflated" (Reuters, "Zepa Almost Empty of Civilians," 28 July 1995); "**Bosnian Army Takes Ukrainian Guns in Enclave**" (15 July); Akashi "Buries Zepa, Says UN Powerless" (19 July). The VRS Žepa attacking force was a tactical group built on the **1st Podrinje Light Infantry Brigade (Rogatica)** and **5th Podrinje Light Infantry Brigade (Višegrad)** (~a battalion each), per ICTY Krstić transcripts 28-30 June 2000. — *BB1 p.446 en.427-441; en.432.*

**F6.3 The distinct Žepa fate (evacuation/internment in Serbia, fewer executions) is NOT described in the extracted KB.** As the prior pass anticipated, the KB's detailed *Žepa* material is largely **1993** (the 1993 Žepa/Podrinje tactical-group content sits in Annex 36 endnotes, `BB2_p0416`). The 1995 Žepa narrative lives in the same missing BB1 chapter and is **NOT IN KB.**

## 7. NOT IN KB — must be sourced elsewhere before any canon use

The following are **not citable from this KB** and would require **separate, out-of-KB sourcing** (authoritative: **ICTY Krstić, IT-98-33** and **Popović et al., IT-05-88** judgements; the ICTY Krstić trial transcripts of 27-30 June 2000 that BB itself relies on; Rohde, *Endgame*; Honig & Both, *Srebrenica*):

1. **The ~8,000 (or 7,000-8,000) massacre / execution death toll.** No aggregate figure appears in the extracted KB. (The "8,000/7,000" string hits are OCR-fragmented troop/artillery/population numbers in unrelated pages, not the massacre toll.)
2. **The legal adjudication of genocide.** "Genocide" appears only twice in the KB: a 1992 context (`BB1_p0198`) and a 1993 *Serbian propaganda* quote ("protecting the Serbian population from genocide," `BB2_p0413`). **No Srebrenica genocide finding is in the KB.**
3. **The detailed column route and day-by-day timeline** (Šušnjari/Buljim → Konjević Polje → Baljkovica → Nezuk), the **column's starting size (~10-15,000)**, **losses en route**, the **Baljkovica corridor / Nezuk breakthrough** (both place-names absent), and **how many reached 2nd Corps / Tuzla lines** as a discrete figure.
4. **Potočari separations, the Kravica warehouse and other execution sites, mass-grave forensics** as narrative (only press-article endnote *titles* survive).
5. **Naser Orić's absence from the enclave in 1995** and the identity of the acting enclave/28th Division commander during the fall.
6. **The Žepa 1995 evacuation-to-Serbia / internment mechanism** and its lower execution rate.

**Bright-line note for authoring:** Because the fall narrative pages are missing and BB's own column/execution facts are secondary renderings of ICTY testimony, **any AWWV Codex text asserting the massacre scale, the genocide finding, or the column's route/casualties must cite ICTY (Krstić / Popović) directly, not this KB.** The KB can rigorously support: the strangle-then-seize *directive* and its June-28 trigger, the *command structure* (Drina Corps / Blagojević / Pandurević, Krstić on trial), the *Potočari flight* and *Žepa attack* dates, and the **2,700-reconstituted / 3,200-unaccounted** reconstitution split — and little else.
