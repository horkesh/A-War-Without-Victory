## 1. Cited Historical Narrative

The Croat-Bosniak war of 1993 (sometimes "war within the war") was a sustained HVO-ARBiH armed conflict in central Bosnia and Herzegovina from January 1993 (Gornji Vakuf, see H2) through the Washington Agreement of March 1994 (B10 / H9 / X5). The war produced a documented sequence of atrocities — almost all individuated by ICTY case law — across both factions. The H5 umbrella family is the AWWV register for **the war state and its engine-driven incidents**. The legal record:

### HVO-perpetrated atrocities (against Bosniak civilians)

- **Ahmići massacre, 16 April 1993** — approximately 116 Bosniak civilians (including 32 women and 11 children) killed by HVO forces (including the "Jokers" / Vitezovi special-police unit) in the village of Ahmići in the Lašva valley; village systematically burned. ICTY findings: **Blaškić Trial Judgment (IT-95-14-T, 3 March 2000) §§383-415**; **Kordić Trial Judgment (IT-95-14/2-T, 26 February 2001) §§631-720**; **Kupreškić et al. Trial Judgment (IT-95-16-T, 14 January 2000) §§129-340** (individual liability of five Kupreškić defendants); **Prlić Trial Judgment (IT-04-74-T, 29 May 2013) Vol. 2 §§385-450** (JCE structural framing).
- **Stupni Do massacre, 23 October 1993** — at least 37-38 Bosniak civilians killed by HVO Bobovac Brigade in the village of Stupni Do near Vareš. ICTY findings: **Kordić Trial Judgment §§720-780**; **Prlić Trial Judgment Vol. 2 §§560-600**. Existing AWWV event at `data/scenarios/events/war_1993.json:2768` (`stupni_do_massacre_1993`).
- **Mostar bridge destruction / Mostar siege incidents 1993-1994** — HVO siege of East Mostar (May 1993 - early 1994); destruction of Stari Most (Old Bridge) 9 November 1993; documented forced displacement and detention. ICTY findings: **Naletilić & Martinović Trial Judgment (IT-98-34-T, 31 March 2003) §§300-470** (Heliodrom detention complex and detention-camp atrocity sequence); **Prlić Trial Judgment Vol. 3 §§600-780** (East Mostar siege and HVO detention infrastructure).
- **Lašva valley campaign (April 1993 — Vitez, Busovača, Kiseljak, Vares)** — connected operations producing forced displacement, civilian casualties, and detention. ICTY findings: **Blaškić §§383-580**, **Kordić §§631-820**, **Prlić Vol. 2 §§385-560**.
- **Sovići / Doljani (Jablanica area), April 1993** — HVO attack on Bosniak villages with detention of approximately 400 civilians and 70-75 ARBiH soldiers; ICTY findings within **Prlić Vol. 2 §§450-490** and **Naletilić §§250-300**. Existing AWWV event at `data/scenarios/events/war_1993.json:1020`.

### ARBiH-perpetrated atrocities (against Croat civilians)

- **Trusina killings, 16 April 1993** (same day as Ahmići) — ARBiH unit kills 15 Croat civilians and 7 HVO POWs in the village of Trusina near Konjic. ICTY findings: case files referenced under regional Konjic command and broader 1993 indictments; existing AWWV event at `data/scenarios/events/war_1993.json:1068` provides the narrative register. Cross-reference to **Prlić Vol. 2** for cross-faction context.
- **Grabovica killings, 8-9 September 1993** — at least 13 Croat civilians killed at Grabovica by ARBiH units from Sarajevo during Operation Neretva 93. ICTY findings: **Halilović Trial Judgment (IT-01-48-T, 16 November 2005)** — Halilović indicted, acquitted due to insufficient evidence of effective command control. Existing AWWV event at `data/scenarios/events/war_1993.json:2592` (`grabovica_uzdol_massacres_1993`) carries the narrative.
- **Uzdol killings, 14 September 1993** — at least 29 Croat civilians and one HVO POW killed at Uzdol during Operation Neretva 93. ICTY findings: same Halilović case-file framing; existing AWWV event `grabovica_uzdol_massacres_1993`.

### Detention-camp exposure (Ring 1/2 chain)

The HVO operated a network of detention facilities in 1993 including **Heliodrom** (Mostar), **Dretelj** (Čapljina), **Gabela** (Čapljina), and **Rodoč**. ICRC and UN documentation, plus ICTY findings at **Naletilić §§250-470** and **Prlić Vol. 3 §§600-780**, establish the systematic detention pattern. The existing H6 row (per packet §4) handles **the exposure / disclosure-response decision** as a separate player-facing row; H5 (this worksheet) records the war state from which detention emerges.

### Operational chronology in BB

BB Vol. II April-November 1993 chronology provides operational context for the cascade: Gornji Vakuf (January 1993, H2) → Lašva valley (April 1993) → Mostar siege (May 1993 onwards) → Operation Neretva 93 (September 1993) → Stupni Do (October 1993) → November 1993 broader operations into Vareš. The Washington Agreement of March 1994 (B10 / H9 / X5) is the historical endpoint.
