## 1. Cited Historical Narrative

On **6 May 1992** in **Graz, Austria**, Radovan Karadžić (President of the self-proclaimed Republika Srpska) and Mate Boban (President of HZ HB) met and issued a joint statement on the territorial organization of Bosnia-Herzegovina. The meeting and the joint statement are extensively documented:

- **Prlić et al. IT-04-74-T Trial Judgment** (29 May 2013) Vol. 1 reconstructs the Graz meeting at §§490-525 within the broader account of HZ HB territorial coordination with Belgrade/Pale. The Trial Chamber treated the meeting as evidence of the JCE's territorial framework — a Croat-Serb understanding on partition lines that left only a residual Bosniak central-Bosnia territory.
- **Kordić IT-95-14/2-T Trial Judgment** (26 February 2001) §§485-510 discusses the Graz statement in the context of Croat-Serb tactical understanding versus active conflict elsewhere on the Bosnian-Serb / Bosnian-Croat frontiers.
- The **Tuđman Presidential Transcripts** (admitted in evidence in Prlić, Kordić, and Blaškić) include Tuđman's reaction to the Graz initiative and his subsequent calibration of Zagreb policy — at points expressing tactical approval of de-escalation with the Serb side to free HVO capacity for territorial consolidation against ARBiH, at other points expressing concern about the diplomatic optics. Cited in Prlić Trial Judgment Vol. 1 §§357-389 and §§510-525.

The Graz meeting is **not** an alliance treaty. It is a **mutual understanding on territorial scope** between two leaderships whose forces continued to engage each other in some sectors (Mostar 1992 HVO defense against the JNA/VRS during June-July 1992 post-dates Graz by weeks). What Graz **did** shift was the institutional ceiling on Croat-Bosniak cooperation: by signaling that HZ HB had a separate territorial track that did not require ARBiH partnership, Graz reduced the political room for sustained HVO-ARBiH alliance.

The **operational consequence** is the sequence of localized frictions through summer-autumn 1992 that the ICTY traced into the 1993 central-Bosnia war:

- **Prozor (October 1992)** — HVO offensive against ARBiH positions in Prozor town; expulsion of Bosniak civilians; documented in Prlić Trial Judgment Vol. 2 §§110-180 as the first sustained HVO-ARBiH armed engagement after Graz, four months after the meeting.
- **Novi Travnik (June-October 1992)** — escalating HVO-ARBiH checkpoint disputes, command-jurisdiction disputes, and small-arms exchanges; Prlić Trial Judgment Vol. 2 §§181-220; Kordić Trial Judgment §§511-555.
- **Vitez (summer-autumn 1992)** — HVO-ARBiH friction over factory access, municipal command structures, and HVO assertion of HZ HB jurisdiction; precursor to the April 1993 Lašva-valley campaign. Kordić Trial Judgment §§556-610; Blaškić Trial Judgment §§99-160 (Operative Zone of Central Bosnia institutional history through 1992).

These are the **historical frictions emerging from a structural Graz baseline**, not from a unitary central-command decision. Prlić Vol. 1-2 frame the post-Graz period as one where HZ HB institutional consolidation, Zagreb logistical leverage, and local HVO-ARBiH disputes converged to make sustained cooperation increasingly difficult — without producing a single Boban-level "break alliance" decision before January 1993.

The historical actor — the HZ HB Presidency in the late spring / summer 1992 window when this row fires — accepted the Graz framework and did not act to prevent the cascade of local frictions. The historical default for H1a is therefore "local frictions emerge and are not suppressed by HZ HB leadership," not "Boban orders open war."

## 2. Defensible Historical/Default Option

- **Label:** `local_friction_emerges` — HZ HB accepts the Graz territorial understanding and allows local commanders in Prozor, Novi Travnik, and Vitez to assert HZ HB jurisdiction; the joint anti-VRS front degrades through dispute, not through a central decision to break.
- **Rationale:** This describes the **emergent historical posture** of the HZ HB Presidency through May-November 1992. The Trial Chambers in Prlić, Kordić, and Blaškić did not find a discrete Boban-level "rupture decision" between Graz and January 1993; they found a structural disposition that permitted (and benefited from) local frictions accumulating into a war. Authoring this as the historical default keeps the option set faithful to the ICTY record.
- **Citation:** Prlić Trial Judgment Vol. 1 §§490-525 (Graz); Vol. 2 §§110-220 (Prozor / Novi Travnik); Kordić Trial Judgment §§485-610 (Graz and Lašva valley 1992); Blaškić Trial Judgment §§99-160 (Operative Zone Central Bosnia 1992); Tuđman Presidential Transcripts as admitted at Prlić (Vol. 1 §§357-389).

## 3. Proposed Counterfactual Options

### Option: `formal_alliance_persists`
- **Label:** `formal_alliance_persists` — HZ HB Presidency actively suppresses local frictions; reinforces the joint anti-VRS framework; treats Graz as an external diplomatic exchange that does not bind HVO operational posture.
- **Historical analogy:** The **Mostar 1992 defense (June-July 1992)** — HVO 4th Brigade and ARBiH units jointly resisting the JNA/VRS Mostar operation — is the operational analogue. It demonstrates that HVO-ARBiH joint anti-VRS operations were feasible at scale when local command and political leadership prioritized them. Counterfactually, sustaining the Mostar 1992 cooperative pattern through Prozor/Novi Travnik/Vitez would require HZ HB Presidency-level intervention to discipline HVO commanders asserting HZ HB jurisdiction.
- **Design provenance:** `design_counterfactual` — the historical record shows the HZ HB Presidency did **not** intervene to suppress local frictions in summer 1992. The branch counterfactually models such intervention. Provenance is grounded in (a) Mostar 1992 operational fact, (b) Kljujić's pre-removal political line as documented in Kordić §§479-490, (c) the existence of HVO commanders (e.g., the Mostar 4th Brigade leadership through July 1992) who treated the anti-VRS front as primary.
- **Sensitive-history check:** Confirmed — option authorizes no atrocity, no detention, no cleansing. Suppressing local frictions is the diplomatic/command intervention; it forecloses the H2 trigger condition by maintaining `alliance_below 0.45` from firing. No Ring 1/2 row is directly authored or altered.
