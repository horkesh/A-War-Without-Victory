## 2. RS Goal Hierarchy

RS first preference is independence or permanent separation; the realistic negotiated target is maximal autonomy inside Bosnia and Herzegovina. The political objective is consistent with the 1992 Bosnian Serb strategic objectives discussed in ICTY Karadzic and Mladic proceedings and with the Dayton settlement that created Republika Srpska as one of two entities inside Bosnia and Herzegovina.

Recommended anchors: ICTY Karadzic IT-95-5/18 trial judgment sections on the Strategic Objectives; ICTY Mladic IT-09-92 trial judgment for leadership objective findings; General Framework Agreement for Peace, Annex 4 and Annex 2 for postwar institutional and territorial settlement; Balkan Battlegrounds for military-diplomatic context.

## 3. HRHB Goal Hierarchy

HRHB first preference is a separate Croat unit or third entity; the realistic negotiated target is strengthened cantonal and Federation protections after the Washington Agreement. This reflects the shift from Croat separatist structures toward the Federation framework before Dayton.

Recommended anchors: ICTY Prlic et al. findings on Herceg-Bosna political project; Washington Agreement, 1994; Dayton Annex 4; Balkan Battlegrounds coverage of Croat-Bosniak conflict and later Federation alignment.

## 4. RBiH Goal Hierarchy

RBiH preference hierarchy centers on Bosnia and Herzegovina state continuity and the strongest feasible state-level institutions. Dayton preserved the state but accepted entity decentralization, so the negotiated target is measured as state continuity plus institutional competences, not total unitary victory.

Recommended anchors: General Framework Agreement for Peace, Annex 4; wartime Bosnian government platform materials where available; ICTY contextual findings on state continuity and siege/enclave context; Balkan Battlegrounds for strategic situation.

## 6. Preconditions

The four minimal precondition levers are historically supportable:

- IVP: Sarajevo, safe areas, humanitarian convoy visibility, and atrocity exposure altered international pressure.
- Patron pressure: Serbia/FRY pressure on RS, Croatia's role over HRHB/HVO, and US/EU pressure on the Bosnian government affected acceptance space.
- Exhaustion: manpower, supply, sanctions, and static fronts narrowed alternatives.
- Army strength / relative balance: 1995 NATO/Federation/ARBiH pressure made Dayton materially different from earlier failed plans.

War crimes are not a negotiation lever. They are judgment and condemnation facts, consistent with the Sensitive History Design Gate.
