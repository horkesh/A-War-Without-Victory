# Command Card Faction Prompt Pack

**Date:** 2026-06-07 (rev. 2 — Category Identity System + army insignia)
**Status:** Working production prompt pack for Gemini command-card art. Supersedes ad hoc chat prompts and the rev. 1 "every card is an office with a flag" set.

## Scope

This pack provides 33 self-contained prompts: 11 command-card subjects for each playable faction (`RBiH`, `RS`, `HRHB`).

Generation filenames use faction suffixes such as `cat_diplomacy_RBiH.webp`. **The runtime resolver is now faction-aware** (`src/ui/map/data/presidentialCommandArt.ts`): it resolves `command_cards/<id>_<faction>.webp` for the active player faction first, then falls back to the faction-agnostic `command_cards/<id>.webp`, then to the shared desk asset. Drop a `<id>_<faction>.webp` in and it wins with no code edit. All 33 cards (11 × RBiH/RS/HRHB) ship in `src/ui/map/assets/command_cards/`.

Category cards are `1024x768` 4:3. Action cards are `1280x720` 16:9. Dimensions are included inside every prompt.

## Why rev. 2 exists

In rev. 1 the nine institutional cards per faction were all built from the same four tokens — corner pole-flag, local newspaper, staff-map, paper stacks — so the category (the thing that should distinguish them) was carried only by weak secondary props. The cards collapsed into "an office with a flag." Convoy and front-visit stood apart only because they dropped those tokens and went to the field.

Rev. 2 fixes this with a **Category Identity System** and by **integrating each faction's army insignia** on the military-character cards.

## Design System

**Category = bones. Faction = skin.**

- **Bones (category):** a distinct *space + hero subject + camera + lighting* per category. These are identical across factions, so every category reads as itself at thumbnail size.
- **Skin (faction):** architecture, script, newspaper, and flag/crest. These change per faction without changing the silhouette.

### Category bones (6 cards, 1024x768 4:3)

| Category | Space | Hero subject (dominates frame) | Camera | Light key |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| War Direction | Operations room | The **map table** — arrows, front-marks, weights, dividers, radio | High three-quarter, looking **down** onto the table | Cool working daylight, lamp pooled on map |
| Command | Seat of authority | The **commander's empty chair** + a small unit desk-standard on the desk | **Frontal, symmetric, eye-level** | Even institutional daylight |
| Diplomacy | Negotiation table | **Two facing empty chairs** down the table; envelopes, glasses, one pennant | Low, **along the table axis** | Cool formal side-window light |
| Home Front | **Supply depot (not an office)** | **Crates, flour sacks, a ration balance-scale**, counter ledgers | Wider, warehouse depth, slightly elevated | Dim utilitarian, single hanging bulb |
| Conscience | Moral / night space | **One lit object in darkness** — open ledger under a single lamp | Tight, intimate, heavy shadow | Night, single source |
| Record | Archive | A **wall of archive shelving / box stacks / card catalog**, typewriter at base | Frontal, facing the shelf wall | Warm dusty raking afternoon light |

### Action bones (5 cards, 1280x720 16:9)

| Action | Hero subject | Camera | Insignia | Flag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Authorize Op | Operation order being stamped (crest as ink stamp + folder emboss) | Close top-down on desk | **Yes — stamp + folder** | Edge only |
| Replace Commander | Empty officer's cap/beret + typed order (crest as cap-badge) | Close, side-lit, low | **Yes — cap-badge** | Edge only |
| Patron Relations | Faction pennant **+ a foreign/neutral pennant**, international correspondence | Eye-level across the table | No | Flag + 2nd pennant |
| Convoy | Distant truck line on terrain | High / behind | No | None (terrain only) |
| Front Visit | Staff car approaching an OP | Behind, distant | No | None (terrain only) |

## Faction Marker Policy

- **RBiH and HRHB — no cloth flags.** Faction identity is carried by a **wall-mounted coat-of-arms plaque**, flat on the wall (RBiH = blue shield with white diagonal band + gold fleurs-de-lis; HRHB = red-and-white checkerboard shield) — plus the unit crest on organic objects in the four insignia cards (folder / desk-standard / stamp / cap-badge). Wall-mounting a flat coat of arms avoids the recurring problem of a flag's shield rendering as a canted or perpendicular decal.
- **RS — keeps the wartime plain tricolor flag** (red-blue-white, no shield, so no decal/cant problem).
- **Field/action scenes** (`act_convoy_*`, `act_front_visit_*`): no flag and no crest — faction read from terrain only.

## Insignia Attachment Protocol

For the four insignia cards (`cat_war_direction_*`, `cat_command_*`, `act_authorize_op_*`, `act_replace_commander_*`) **attach the faction crest image alongside the text prompt**:

- RBiH → `crest_ARBiH.webp`
- RS → `crest_VRS.webp`
- HRHB → `crest_HVO.webp`

Each insignia prompt embeds this guard so Gemini reproduces your art instead of inventing heraldry:

```text
Use the attached image as the exact reference for the unit insignia, and place it ONLY on a small, believable object the army actually marks — never as a framed crest hung on the wall. Reproduce its shape, emblem, and lettering faithfully — do not redraw, restyle, simplify, or re-letter it — but render it small, worn, and color-graded into the scene's faded, desaturated, low-contrast palette so it reads as a real period object rather than a clean bright badge, never glossy. It must be subordinate to the scene's hero object. Do not enlarge it, do not add any other crest, flag-emblem, or coat of arms, do not hang it on the wall, and do not place the insignia anywhere else in the frame.
```

Context-specific mounts (organic objects, never wall-framed): War Direction = printed emblem on a closed operations folder lying on the desk; Command = a small cast-brass unit desk-standard on the desk in front of the empty chair, no taller than the telephone; Authorize Op = a single-color ink stamp impression (pečat) on the order plus a worn printed emblem on the folder cover; Replace Commander = metal cap-badge on the beret/cap.

**Don't switch to clean vector/SVG crests.** A flat vector renders crisper, flatter, and more saturated, so it pops out of the photographic scene worse, not better. The aged cast-metal `.webp` crests are the right source — they already carry weathering and relief that help them sit in-scene. Integration problems ("the crest stands out / is too big") are fixed with the scale + color-grade language above, not with a cleaner source file.

## Faction Flag Clauses

**RBiH faction-marker clause:** RBiH cards show the Republic of Bosnia and Herzegovina coat of arms as a flat wall-mounted plaque (painted or low-relief) — the blue shield with a white diagonal band and gold fleurs-de-lis. No cloth flag. Not the post-1998 Bosnia and Herzegovina flag.

**RBiH edge-flag short form:** *(retired — RBiH now uses a wall-mounted coat of arms, no cloth flag).*

**RS flag clause:** The RS flag must be the wartime Republika Srpska plain tricolor: three horizontal stripes, red on top, blue in the middle, white on the bottom. No crest, no emblem, no eagle, no post-Dayton symbolism.

**HRHB faction-marker clause:** HRHB cards show the Herzeg-Bosna coat of arms as a flat wall-mounted plaque (painted or low-relief) — the red-and-white checkerboard shield. No cloth flag. Not the Republic of Croatia flag and not the Croatian national coat of arms.

## Map Clauses

Use generic warroom maps only. Any visible map must be a close-cropped topographic staff-map fragment with terrain shading, contour lines, grid ticks, road/river-like thin lines, grease-pencil arrows, and small colored front marks. It must show large local detail, not a full country. Do not show the outline of Bosnia and Herzegovina, do not show municipality borders, do not show city names, do not show labels, do not show post-1995 Federation/Republika Srpska entity lines, do not show Dayton-style boundary lines, and do not show any recognizable modern political border. If Gemini cannot keep the map generic, make the map partially covered by tracing paper, cropped at the edges, and unreadable.
