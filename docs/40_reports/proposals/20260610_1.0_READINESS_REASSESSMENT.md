## 1. What the path WAS vs what today changes

### 1.1 The path as ratified (DoD 2026-06-08, re-sequenced 2026-06-09)

The 2026-06-09 owner amendment put a NEW system in front of the playtest:

> **collapse build (Phase I disabled → II coupling → III enable) → final calibration (re-floored AROUND the collapse engine, off the 649 GUARD) → D2 full-campaign playtest → D3 operator gate → 1.0.**

Rationale at the time: the 649 floor is a regression GUARD not the final tune; collapse is the "negative-sum soul-system" that moves territory; calibrating before it existed would mean re-flooring twice. So D1 (the single calibration finalization) was deliberately *folded into* a post-collapse re-floor that would precede the playtest. **This is the assumption today overturns.**

### 1.2 What today (2026-06-10) actually found

The collapse pipeline was built end-to-end, §6-safe, default-off, and **measured**:

- **Phase I (#375):** internals + §6 guard G1 + 17 constants, DISABLED, byte-identical 40w/188w.
- **Phase IV-a (#381):** unit reconciliation worked — collapse now *fires* Tier-0 (HRHB eligible ~t60) — but **territory 649→649 byte-identical ON vs OFF**, **§6 PASS** (rupture turn 162 identical ON/OFF, 0/9 enclaves accrue damage/modifier). The settlement-keyed Phase 3D substrate writes nothing in OSID-native scenarios.
- **Panel + probe finding:** collapse-as-a-TERRITORY-mechanism is **REDUNDANT** — the sim already reproduces the *one* historical territorial collapse (western Bosnia 1995) via triggered operations (Storm/Mistral/Sana). `local_strain` is a cumulative siege-duration integral that is **anti-correlated** with the real collapse (it peaks where fronts HELD — Sarajevo/Goražde/Tuzla — not where they broke). Driving territory off it would be historically backwards.

**Owner decision (2026-06-10):** repurpose the (already-built, §6-safe) pipeline to the **exhaustion / political-collapse FEEL**, not territory:
- **Design A** — war-weariness read-model + Chronicle beats off `war_exhaustion[fid]/100`, reusing the band module. **Calibration-INERT** (no combat input, no territory, no hash move). Merging as **PR #402** (currently on a branch; `war_weariness_bands.ts` not yet on `main` working tree; the Chronicle beat fires once at the crossing turn, territory byte-identical).
- **Design B** — a faction-own-offense exhaustion DRAG (op-launch willingness, `plan.ts:279`), default-off flag, ONE lever / ONE site, re-scaling an *already-present, already-in-the-649-floor, degenerate* `factionExhaustionDrag` onto the recovered-0..100 scale. Build-plan ready (`20260610_DESIGN_B_BUILD_PLAN.md`). Floor-MOVING but bounded; §6-SAFE (the rupture ops are *triggered*, structurally exempt from intent scoring).

### 1.3 Does "final calibration around collapse" still gate D2?

**No.** The premise of that gate — that collapse is a large territory mover requiring a re-floor before the playtest is meaningful — is falsified. Concretely:

| Re-sequence assumption (2026-06-09) | Reality (2026-06-10) |
|---|---|
| Collapse moves territory; calibrate before D2 so the playtest sees the final engine | Collapse moves **0** territory; the engine the playtest would see is **already** the 649 engine |
| "Final calibration around collapse" is a big re-floor absorbing D1 | The only candidate hash-mover left is Design B's **single bounded lever** (optional, anchor-safe, or skip entirely) |
| D1 is folded *into* the post-collapse re-floor | D1 reverts to what the original DoD said: a **single, small, owner-signed finalization re-floor** — and it may be a near-no-op if Design B is shelved |

**The engine is effectively calibration-STABLE at 649 right now.** The 649 floor is the engine a D2 playtest would experience today (Design A is inert; Design B is default-off until activated). The remaining calibration question is no longer "re-floor around a new territory system" — it is the narrow, pre-existing one: **do we adopt the ONE optional Design-B re-floor before freeze, or freeze at 649?** Either way it is one decision, not a program.
