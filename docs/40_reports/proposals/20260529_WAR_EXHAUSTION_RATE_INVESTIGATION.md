## 4. Quantification — ~50× confirmed and explained

The "~50×" is the ratio of the **fresh rescaled turn-40 values to the stale cap-100 fossil**, which directly reflects the 100× cap raise *minus* the fact that turn-40 has not yet hit the new 10000 cap:

| Faction | J3 stale save (old cap=100) | Current main (rescaled) | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| HRHB | 100.001 | 4752.92 | 47.5× |
| RBiH | 100.001 | 5366.64 | 53.7× |
| RS | 100.001 | 7942.94 | 79.4× |

The 100× cap raise sets the ceiling; at turn 40 the accumulator has reached ~47-79× the old cap (not yet 100×) because the new 10000 cap is not yet saturated and faction-specific front counts diverge. The Track B "~50-79×" band is **confirmed and refined**: it is the rescale ratio, and the spread (47.5 / 53.7 / 79.4) is the *intended* faction differentiation the rescale was designed to restore. RS highest = most sustained static-front + supply pressure.

`exhaustion/3` term in the cohesion formula (`strategic_dimensions.ts:111`): HRHB 1584, RBiH 1789, RS 2647 — each dwarfs the maximum positive contribution (`allianceVal ≤ 40` + `avgCohesion/2 ≤ 50` = 90), so `clamp(.., 0, 100)` floors all three cohesion bases at 0. Confirmed.

The forensics memo rescaled the *gate threshold* consumers but missed this *linear-term* consumer. Neither was in the 2026-05-22 rescale's downstream list.

Net: **the rescale was intended; the cohesion-formula saturation it caused was an overlooked consumer.** J3's ~100 reading that hid this was itself an artifact of a stale save, which is why the saturation only surfaced post-recanon.
