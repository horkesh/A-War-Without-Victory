## 2026-11-17 — `supply_below`/`supply_above` conditions, `supply_delta` effect, 18 reserve-only events and `auto_resolve_turns` retired (Fable consult 6 follow-up; hash result below)

- **Source:** Fable consult 6 follow-up to PR (b) (#801). Supply is connection, not stock: with the reserve layer gone, `supply_below`/`supply_above` read a value that was never written under any apr1992 scenario, and `supply_delta` wrote a value nothing read. About 20 authored events carried them and shipped inert.
- **Event-by-event rulings** (historian, narrative and engine seats, two rounds; the canon seat reviewed the doc edits): 18 consequence events could only fire on the reserve and were deleted: `csq_black_market_supply_route`, `csq_arms_pipeline_disrupted` (+`_RBiH`, `_HRHB`), `csq_supply_corridor_chronic_strain` (+`_RS`, `_HRHB`, `_RBiH`), `csq_winter_supply_attrition` (+`_RBiH`, `_HRHB`, `_RS`), `csq_spring_thaw_supply_recovery_RBiH`/`_RS`/`_HRHB`, `csq_grain_corridor_reopened` (+`_RS`, `_HRHB`). The other events lost their `supply_delta` lines (77 in all). Where an event's primary `effect` was a `supply_delta`, the next real effect took the primary slot (10 events; for eight the first mechanical effect, with the narrative line staying in `effects[]` so the event card still shows it, since a narrative primary beside an effects list is hidden; for `arms_embargo_impact_1992` and `operation_sharp_guard_1993`, which have no other effect, the narrative line itself): `arms_embargo_impact_1992`, `jna_withdrawal_1992`, `croatia_herceg_bosna_control_1993`, `un_resolution_820_sanctions_1993`, `operation_sharp_guard_1993`, `sarajevo_tunnel_completed_1993`, `sarajevo_exclusion_zone_1994`, `belgrade_embargo_rs_1994`, `nato_deliberate_force_1995`, `rapid_reaction_force_1995`. The narrative seat's English copy edits (62 matches) remove every claim of a supply stock, shipment or depot; one wording ("camp") was changed so the sensitive-text carve-out stays complete.
- **Code:** the vocabulary, types and `apply_effects` lose all three kinds; the loader rejects them. An old save's staged decision that still carries a `supply_delta` effect loads and resolves as a silent no-op (`tests/event_decisions.test.ts`). `evaluateCondition` still has `default: return true` for unknown condition types, so the loader rejection and the deletion of the 18 events had to land together; `tests/events_supply_retired.test.ts` guards the catalog.
- **`auto_resolve_turns` retired** (sweep item from the orchestrator: nothing in `src/` read it): 8 events carried `auto_resolve_turns: 2` and promised an auto-resolve of an unanswered decision. The engine never did that and no UI showed a countdown or promise. The field is removed from the type and the 8 events; the loader now rejects it (`tests/event_auto_resolve_retired.test.ts`).
- **UI:** event cards and the decision modal no longer render a supply line (`supply_delta` is a non-display kind for old saves); the turn-aftermath tiles, dispatch lines and AAR panel lose the supply figures (they were constant or derived from the dead reserve). Ten i18n keys removed from both locales.
- **Canon** (same change): Systems Manual (event condition table, §14.2c), Engine Invariants, Rulebook (condition-type count), Game Bible, `EVENT_SYSTEM_AUTHORING_GUIDE.md`, and SB2 in `docs/40_reports/governance/20261115_FINAL_BEHAVIOUR_AT_1_0.md`.
- **Tests:** retired-event tests deleted or rewritten across 31 files; `tests/codex_sensitive_claim_inventory.test.ts` pins `consequences.json` at 483 claims (558 minus 75; the 75 were measured against the 18 deleted subject ids, not taken from the received value).
- **Finding, not fixed here:** the `corridor_severed` condition runs its BFS over settlement edges, not OSID edges, so it is vacuously true for an OSID pair. No shipped event uses it. Reported to the orchestrator for a ruling (retire the condition or fix its edge input).
- **Not touched:** the constant "Supply 100%" operation-readiness rows and the BCS `zalihe` terms stay for a follow-up; the patron-defiance "cost is borne at the front" copy belongs to washington-2.
