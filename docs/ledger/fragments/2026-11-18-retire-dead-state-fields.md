## 2026-11-18 — Retired-field sweep: dead state left by retired systems is removed from GameState, the validators, the turn summary, the aftermath view and the tools

Freeze-cut item. The supply-reserve economy (Fable consult 6, 2026-11-17), humanitarian convoys (F1i, 2026-11-16), the siege counters (§6 panel, 2026-11-17) and the brigade reposition and AoR-cap bridges (April 2026) were retired from the engine but their state fields stayed in `GameState` as optional "compatibility-only" residue. No live code wrote any of them and no live code read them; the only writer left was the v27 migration step, which re-creates the two empty convoy arrays on every serialize (`serializeState` re-runs every step from v1). Every new save therefore carried `pending_convoy_decisions: []` and `convoy_decision_history: []`, and every turn summary carried three always-empty supply sub-objects. The sweep removes the fields themselves.

**Removed (source, mechanical half).**
- `MilitaryState`: `pending_convoy_decisions`, `convoy_decision_history`, `siege_turn_counters`, `general_supply_reserve`, `heavy_munitions_reserve`, `smuggling_routes`, `smuggling_allocation`, `airdrop_allocation`, `brigade_reposition_orders`, `brigade_desired_aor_cap`. The types `SmugglingRouteState`, `PendingConvoyDecision` and `ConvoyDecisionRecord` go with them.
- `StateMeta.supply_reserves_enabled` and the comment-only mentions of it in `src` (runner, loader, scenario types, combat notes).
- `CorpsOperation.artillery_preparation_consumed` (declared, never written or read).
- `TurnSummary.supply_deltas`, `heavy_munitions_deltas`, `supply_snapshot` and `AARSnapshot.supply` / `.heavy_munitions`; `compileSupplyDeltas` and the supply half of the end-of-turn snapshot compiler (now `compileTerritorySnapshot`). Always `{}` before.
- Their consumers: the turn-aftermath view's `supply` block and the cost view's `ownSupplySpent` / `ownHeavyMunitionsSpent` (severity bands and the "supply spent" reason that could never fire), the i18n key `turnAftermath.cost.reason.supplySpent` (EN and BCS), the player-visible projection of the three summary fields and of `brigade_reposition_orders`, the validators and their dispatch for every removed `MilitaryState` field, the `game_state_field_classification` compat list (`dead: []` unchanged), the `event_presidential_acceptance` state literal, the browser-proof tool literals and `tools/fix_literals.ts` key list.
- Kept on purpose: `negotiation_offers` `rationale.supply_snapshot` (live), `TurnSummary.supply_transitions`, `military.strategic_reserves`, the v27 `VERSION_REQUIRED_FIELDS` convoy rows (retired by the migration step, not here), the `supply_delta` filter in `apply_effects.ts` (goes with the migration step).

**Candidates checked.** `brigade_reposition_orders` had a validator and a projection and no sim reader or writer (the `apply-brigade-reposition` step was retired 2026-04-03); `brigade_desired_aor_cap` had a validator only. Both stripped. The adapter's `repositionOrders` view is always empty and is left for the dead-UI sweep.

**Docs (same change).** Engine Invariants (the supply-is-connection and siege-retirement notes), Systems Manual (the CorpsOperation note, the siege-retirement note, §14.2, §14.2a, §14.2c), `context.md`, `REPO_MAP.md`, `DESKTOP_GUI_IPC_CONTRACT.md` and `TACTICAL_MAP_SYSTEM.md` now say the fields were removed from GameState (2026-11-18) instead of "optional, compatibility-only, kept so old saves load". Dated reports, the ledger archive and `life_lessons` are history and are left as written.

**Not in this change (lane owner, migration step).** The save migration that drops the fields from old saves (including the strip of staged `supply_delta` effects in `pending_event_decisions` and of the three turn-summary fields), the schema bump and pins, the `retiredAtVersion` on the two convoy `VERSION_REQUIRED_FIELDS` rows, the new fixture, `SAVE_SCHEMA_EVOLUTION.md`, the startup snapshot rebuild, the drift audit regeneration, the baselines manifest re-pin and the `negotiation_capital` digests. Draft of the strip logic: `_logs/l7-events/sweep_migration_draft.ts`.
