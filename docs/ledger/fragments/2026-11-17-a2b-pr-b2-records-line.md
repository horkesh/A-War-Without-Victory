## 2026-11-17 — A2b PR-B2: the HRHB President sees the refugee compact: one state line in Army HQ Records, in five phases, EN and BCS (hash-neutral; rendered proof)

- **Why.** The #768 merge gate and the PR-B design round found the compact invisible: a standing arrangement the player pays for (recruitment, Zagreb's confidence) with no line that names it, says what it eases, how long it runs or how it ends. PR-B put the compact's price and lapse conditions on the cards where the President decides; this is the standing line the orchestrator asked for, so the compact and its ending are visible between decisions.
- **Change.**
  - `src/sim/early_war/refugee_compact.ts`: `getRefugeeCompactPhase(state, turn)` (pure, built on the same replay as the multipliers; the replay is now an internal helper and `getRefugeeCompactStatus` is unchanged in shape and value): `none`, `active` (with the latest expiry), `suspended` (the displacement damping is lifted after a bilateral flip; the price and the held-back Zagreb pull continue), `lapsed` (the President's own answer, with its turn), `ran_out` (no answer renewed it, with the last expiry) and `signed` (the compact was in force when the Washington Agreement was signed). A compact that had already ended before the signing keeps its own ending.
  - `src/ui/map/data/refugeeCompactLine.ts`: `buildRefugeeCompactLine(state)`: null for every faction but HRHB, for a President with no granting choice, for a bot-sourced grant and without live state; dates through the locale-aware `turnToDateString`.
  - `src/ui/map/components/army_hq/RecordsContent.tsx`: one persistent line under the archive summary (`data-testid="records-refugee-compact"`, `data-compact-phase`), the same gold-rule treatment as the staff reading of policy. An ending stays visible; nothing ends silently.
  - `src/desktop/player_visible_state.cjs`: the projection carries `washington_signed`, `washington_turn` and `refugee_compact_suspended_until` from `political.rbih_hrhb_state` under their own key, `political.refugee_compact_view`, and nothing else of the bilateral state; the key is omitted when none of the three exists. It is deliberately NOT a partial `rbih_hrhb_state`: the war-room diplomacy extractor (`war_data_extractor.ts`) treats that object as complete and crashed on a partial one (caught by CI: `desktop_load_sector_ratings` and the packaged-runtime probe), so a test pins that no `rbih_hrhb_state` leaves.
