## 2026-11-14 — Third-party notices and visible OpenStreetMap attribution

Pre-publication licensing. The new root `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` lists every npm package that ships: 472 name/version pairs, one per lockfile entry outside the dev tree (the root production dependencies, the tactical-map workspace dependencies that Vite bundles, and their transitive dependencies), plus Electron (the runtime) and Tailwind (its Preflight CSS ships). Each row gives the licence and the copyright line. An appendix holds each distinct licence and NOTICE text once. `node tools/licensing/generate_third_party_notices.mjs` writes the file; it is sorted, has no timestamps and uses LF, and `--check` reports a stale file. Platform-specific native binaries take their parent package's record, so the output is the same on every OS. `tests/third_party_notices.test.ts` fails when a shipped package is missing or stale, and when the file differs from a fresh generation. Positive control: deleting the real `pmtiles@3.2.1` row turned it red. The hand-written part (`tools/licensing/third_party_notices_static.md`) covers the fonts (IBM Plex and Caveat under OFL 1.1; the Open Sans map glyphs) and OpenStreetMap data under ODbL 1.0 (`osm.pmtiles`, the OSM road and waterway snapshots and the terrain scalars built from them, the geoBoundaries ADM0 outlines). It also covers Copernicus GLO-30 elevation (`terrain.pmtiles`, `hillshade.pmtiles` and the DEM clip; the two named geography reports record only "external", and the source comes from the 2026-02-28 handover and the 2026-03-20 terrain report), geoBoundaries (CC BY 4.0), the ETOPO 2022 and Natural Earth sources of the opening texture, and the VSCO 2 CE samples (CC0, `docs/audio/LICENSES/`).

The war map and the minimap now carry a 12px "© OpenStreetMap contributors" plate in the dark NATO ops style (near-black, green hairline, gold text). In a browser it links to openstreetmap.org/copyright. In Electron it is plain text, because the main process has no external-link handler. A rendered check on the browser dev map (tracked RS save; 1920×1080 EN, 1366×768 BCS) placed the war-map plate bottom-left on the legend column, clear of the Find button. The default Inbox rail hides the whole minimap plate; when a left rail replaces the Inbox, the minimap plate shows and the war-map plate is covered. The Credits screen gains a Map Data section with the ODbL line (BCS "© OpenStreetMap saradnici"). The same check found that Credits, opened from the main menu, rendered behind it (MODAL_HARD 8500 under HARD_MODAL 9000) and that its backdrop button covered the panel. Credits now uses HARD_MODAL like Settings, and its panel is positioned above the backdrop.

No sim, data or packaging change. Still open: packaged builds do not yet include the notices file, because packaging is paused. Not recorded and needing owner confirmation: the Copernicus attribution wording, the Open Sans glyphs' upstream, the origin of `bih_adm3_1990.geojson`, and the licence of `@mapbox/jsonlint-lines-primitives` (none declared).
