## 2026-11-23 — §6 mechanics items 5 + 6 + 4 (+ item 7 pin): the Observer drain is reachable, answers peace-plan cards like fire time, and leftover paramilitary requests leave the desk when the side stops being manual

§6 mechanics batch, ruled unanimous by six independent seats (game designer, engine, red team, historian, operations, scenario tester; notes `s6_mech_r3_final.md`). Items 4, 5 and 6 are MUST-FIX before the engine freeze and ship in one change; item 7 is RETIRED with a pin test. The Observer copy line went through a §4 copy panel (historian + narrative designer), unanimous in round 2.

**Change.**
- One predicate, `isObserverDrainablePendingDecision` (new leaf `src/sim/events/observer_drain.ts`), read by both the Observer drain (`evaluate_events.ts`) and the player-decision manifest. At Observer a pending card whose authored default is among its own options, and which is not an opening card answered ahead of its firing, no longer hard-blocks Advance; the drain answers exactly those cards at the next evaluate-events. Cards with no authored default, and unfired opening cards, keep blocking (item 6). Before this the drain was unreachable in the product: a required card queued at levels 0-2 held Advance shut at Observer.
- The drain chooses through `selectBotEventResponse(..., true)`, the fire-time Observer selector: a routed peace-plan card follows a plan answer already on record for that side instead of the card's raw default. Every other card gets its authored default, as before (item 5).
- A paramilitary request blocks Advance only while its faction is manual (`selectManualPlayerFaction`). When the player's side is no longer manual, its undecided requests are deleted WITHOUT history (`removeNonManualPlayerParamilitaryRequests`): at `apply-autonomy-transition` in the advance that raises the level, and in detect for saves already at levels 1-3. The staff default acts on those targets at the next detect (item 4).
- Item 7 pin: `meta.player_faction` is assigned only at campaign startup, harness init, save migration and the legacy warroom start.
- Observer copy (`autonomy.level.observer.desc` and `.descNoCard`, EN and BCS): "A decision with no historical response still waits for you" was false at fire time (17 required cards with no authored default, among them the Drina decision, are answered by the staff). New text: "A decision with no historical response that comes up at Observer is answered by your staff. If one was already on your desk when you chose Observer, it stays there until you answer it."
- `tests/paramilitary_policy_binding.test.ts`: the carried-request pin is restated to the item 4 ruling (a carried request is never approved and writes no history; the staff then follow the order in force).
