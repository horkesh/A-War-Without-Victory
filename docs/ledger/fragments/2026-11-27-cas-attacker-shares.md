## 2026-11-27 — CAS-ATTACKER-SHARES + OP-CAS-LEAD: battle records, weekly_report rows and operation records carry each attacker's own applied loss

Authority: orchestrator rulings §42 (CAS-ATTACKER-SHARES, MUST-FIX, pre-freeze, reporting only) and §47 (OP-CAS-LEAD folded in as one records-only change). Carries the two §41 FARZ-END-RECEIPT regression tests (`tests/farz_end_reopen.test.ts`, from e179683cc). Hash-moving (records only on the measured runs).

**Change.**
- The resolver's battle record gains `attacker_contributions`: each attacker's own applied loss (its casualty share plus any morale-absorption extra), sorted by brigade id. A TG anchor's entry includes the loss of the men its donors lent it, since they fought inside the TG, so the entries sum to the stack total. Brigade history still books each donor its own loss (CAS-REPORT-4, §22). It is report-only: `compile_turn_summary` does not copy it, so no GameState field changes.
- `weekly_report.jsonl` battle rows now always carry `attacker_brigades` and `attacker_contributions` (`weeklyAttackerStackFields`). `attacker_brigades` was emitted only under the `battle_stack` reason-code topic; `defender_contributions` stays under that topic.
- `attacker_casualties` is documented, on the record and the weekly row, as the whole stack's applied loss, not `attacker_brigade`'s (the lead).
- OP-CAS-LEAD (§47), `attributeOperationCasualties`: each attacking brigade's own applied loss is credited to its own operation, and each defending brigade's to its own, with its share of what the stack inflicted and destroyed and its own equipment estimate. Before, the whole stack's loss went to the lead attacker's operation. Totals are preserved exactly: shares in strictCompare brigade order, the remainder placed by a stated rule (`sharesPreservingTotal`, largest remainder in `apportionLargestRemainder`). A one-brigade side gives the old figures. `op.pending_casualties` drains into `op.weekly_log`, which only reports and the player's operation detail read.
- Player-visible surfaces were audited. The AAR panel labels its loss line by side and lists the whole stack, so it does not change. LETTER-LEAD is accepted as final (§47).
- Tests: `tests/attacker_casualty_applied_record.test.ts` (each attacker's entry equals its personnel drop, and the entries sum to the stack total; TG anchor), `tests/weekly_battle_attacker_stack_fields.test.ts`, `tests/op_cas_lead.test.ts`. All were red on main.
