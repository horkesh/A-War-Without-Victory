## 2026-11-27 — TG donor bookkeeping: a donor's loss counter books what was removed, and each brigade's history records its own loss

Authority: orchestrator rulings §17 (TG-LOAN, R10) and §22 (CAS-REPORT-4), queued as one records PR (§38, §43). Seats: systems GO (donor-cap wording applied), plus the lane C s18 seats on the original branches. Hash-moving (records only on the measured runs).

**Change.**
- TG-LOAN: a tactical group's donor loss counter (`casualties_so_far`) adds `applyPersonnelLoss`'s return value, so a donor clamped at its floor books the men it actually lost, not its planned share. That counter feeds the donor cap, the removable room, dissolve telemetry and the AAR "N lost" line.
- CAS-REPORT-4: the resolver keeps a TG anchor's own applied loss apart from its donors'. The anchor's brigade-history row holds its own loss (plus absorption extra). `recordTgDonorLoss` books each donor's applied loss in its own history (total, worst single battle, nadir, open elite-loan episode tally) with no engagement row and no `battles_fought`. `compile_turn_summary` sums per-cell casualties from the battle records, so donor loss stays in the turn summary.
- Canon: Systems Manual tactical-group donors paragraph. FORAWWV unchanged.
- Tests: `tests/attacker_casualty_applied_record.test.ts` (a donor 3 above its floor books 3, not 31; anchor row = its own drop, donor total = its drop with no battle credit).
