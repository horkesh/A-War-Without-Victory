## 2026-11-16 — Bright line: the human player's own atrocity carries the consequences in every decision mode

**Why.** The §6 bright line ("atrocity must never produce a better end-state") was enforced by mode: the `authorized_cleansing_condemnation` flag, its terminal-turn display freshening and the atrocity term of the legacy war-cost index applied only when `decision_mode === 'emergent'`, while the counters that feed them are written in both modes. "Replay the historical record" is one click in the New Game menu, so a player could pick the mode in which the same acts cost nothing (synthetic state, same inputs: historical = no flag, grade A, survival; emergent = flag, grade C, hollow_victory). Owner principle (2026-11-16): the game reproduces history if left alone and lets the player make it ahistorical; consequences follow the player's own acts in either mode.
