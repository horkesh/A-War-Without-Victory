## 2026-11-15 — Command loop L3c: a Level-1 plan with no objective is released, not held for an answer that cannot come

- **Symptom (found in the #642 live Assisted run).** `arbih_1st_corps` held `plan_arbih_1st_corps_t15_pre_planned_op` (source `pre_planned`, `target_osids: []`) at `ready` from turn 15 to the end. `generateLevel1OpProposals` skips plans without targets, so it was never proposed, never expired, never released, and no new plan could start: a second route into the Council's L8 stuck-plan symptom.
- **Root cause.** `tryCreateFromPrePlanned` (`commander/plan.ts`) reads `briefing.pre_planned_ops[0]` as an operation definition, but the briefing passes `corps_command.queued_operations`, a list of operation names (`string[]`). The adopted plan gets the fallback name `pre_planned_op` and `extractTargetOsids` returns `[]`. This holds for every faction: a bot corps' plan of this kind emits no operation (a non-opportunity plan needs targets) and is cleared on its next pass, so bots cycle through dead plans rather than stick.
- **Fix (player path only, headless-inert).** One rule, `isLevel1ProposablePlan` (`bot/staff_control.ts`), now decides both what `generateLevel1OpProposals` proposes and what the Level-1 guard in `applyCommanderOutput` may hold. A player plan the President cannot be asked about is released (no operation; the commander replans), as the bot's identical plan is cleared. Engine Invariants §14.10b updated.
- **Not fixed (reported).** Making the commander resolve queued names into real targets, or stop adopting them, changes bot plan-slot use in headless runs: a measured calibration change, left to the orchestrator.
- **Tests.** `tests/sim/autonomy/level1_unproposable_plan_release.test.ts` (shared rule; never proposed; player released; a plan with targets still held; bot and headless unchanged). Level-1 fixtures in `level1_player_only_gate` and `level1_expired_proposal_release` now carry targets, since they stand for proposable plans.
- **Inertness.** See the PR: equal 40-week headless artifact hashes against main and a live strip-and-compare. Evidence under `C:/Pyrrhic/AWWV-worktrees/_logs/cl-l3c-empty-target-20261115/`.
