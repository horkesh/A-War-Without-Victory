## 2026-11-17 — Supply-reserve economy, airdrop action, smuggling allocation and `jan1993_to_dayton` retired (Fable consult 6 PR (b); hash-neutral)

- **Source:** Fable consult 6, appendix A, PR (b). Supply is modelled as connection, not stock: a formation is supplied if it can be reached; there are no depots, ammunition stocks or patron shipments.
- **Why it is safe:** the whole reserve layer ran only under the scenario flag `supply_reserves_enabled`. No apr1992 scenario sets it. Only `sep1991_to_dayton` and `jan1993_to_dayton` did, and no player can start either (the desktop New Game accepts only `apr_1992`; one start for 1.0). The airdrop button wrote root `state.airdrop_allocation`, which nothing read: a player action that did nothing.
- **Removed:**
  - The reserve module and constants (`supply_reserves.ts`, `supply_reserve_constants.ts`), smuggling routes (`smuggling_routes.ts`), the reserve-blended effective supply state, the heavy-munitions scaling of bombardment and artillery suppression, `computeSupplyReadiness`, and the artillery-preparation reserve spend (the toggle still records on the operation).
  - The pipeline steps `compute-supply-reserves` and `update-smuggling-routes` (war step count 191 to 189).
  - The `stage-airdrop-allocation` IPC with its preload and `useIPC` entries, the Economy panel, the reserve rows of the Logistics panel (SupplyPanel stays: corridor counts and the Sarajevo lifeline indicator), the airdrop control of the Enclave dashboard, the Army HQ reserve section and 42 i18n keys per locale.
  - The `supply_reserves_enabled` scenario key (now ignored) and `data/scenarios/jan1993_to_dayton.json` (J1).
- **Kept:** the reserve, smuggling and airdrop fields stay optional, compatibility-only save fields so old saves load (`tests/supply_reserves_retired.test.ts`); they go in the retired-field sweep. Enclave resilience and hardening stay (constants moved to `enclave_resilience_constants.ts`, values unchanged). The embargo offensive-launch gate stays (it never read a reserve). Equipment smuggling in `faction_progression.ts` is a different system and stays. The `supply_below`/`supply_above` event conditions and the `supply_delta` effect are inert and are retired in the supply-events change.
- **Merge with main:** #739 retired the convoys and the Sarajevo tunnel activation; main won on both. `humanitarian_convoys.ts` and the moved tunnel activator are not in this change. The war step count is 189.
- **Guard added:** `tests/ipc_write_paths_have_readers.test.ts` scans every desktop IPC handler write and requires an engine reader. It found six root-level writes with no reader; they are reported to the orchestrator and listed in the test as known defects until each is retired or fixed (see the dead-IPC change).
