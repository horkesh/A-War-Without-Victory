## 2026-11-26 — P3-EST-PRIM: the force-ratio estimate is read at the axes' current objective

Authority: the orchestrator's freeze-inventory rulings 2026-11-26 §13 (MUST-FIX before the freeze; its own measured pair after #877). Hash-moving.

**Change.**
- `estimateForceRatio` (`src/sim/combat/operation_preparation.ts`) took its primary objective as the lowest-sorted objective of the whole list. In 19 of 104 operations on the #877 fix pair that was a depth objective (the operations seat's check: Vlasic Ridge read at gornje_krcevine while its first battle is at Paklarevo; Grmeč 94 at orasac_2 rather than ripac).
- With live axes, the primary is now the lowest-sorted of the axes' first contested objectives: per axis, the first objective in the preparation view, from the current index on, that neither the attacker nor an ally holds and that is not uncontrolled (`firstContestedAxisObjective`). Without one, the lowest objective as before. The simultaneous-axes divisor (`countOperationAxesOnDefendingSector`) counts the same cells.
- The contested-cell rule is the operations seat's change (lane B s24). The estimate runs in preparation, before the launch reconcile drops friendly-held objectives. On the first pair, Farz 95 read vozuca_2 after the 211th had taken it, and fell to the no-defender sentinel.
- Canon: Systems Manual, "Force-ratio estimate". FORAWWV unchanged.
- Tests: `tests/p3_est_force_ratio.test.ts`. A lower-sorted depth objective is not the primary, and advancing the axis moves it. An attacker-held current objective is skipped, and so is an uncontrolled one. With no contested objective from the index on, the lowest objective is read.
