## Deliverables

### Phase 1: Production → Reserve Wiring (~0.5 session)
**Assigned to:** Systems Programmer

The 7 facilities compute a production bonus but it never feeds into reserves. Wire it.

- [ ] **Wire production income** — in `updateSupplyReserves()` (or new pipeline step), add production facility output to faction reserves each turn:
  ```
  for each facility controlled by faction:
    general_reserve += facility.base_capacity × facility.condition × PRODUCTION_GENERAL_RATE
    heavy_reserve += facility.base_capacity × facility.condition × PRODUCTION_HEAVY_RATE
  ```
  Constants: `PRODUCTION_GENERAL_RATE = 0.3`, `PRODUCTION_HEAVY_RATE = 0.1`
- [ ] **Facility damage wiring** — verify `condition` degrades when battles occur in facility municipality (already 0.05/battle — just confirm it runs)
- [ ] **Tests** — production income increases reserves, damage reduces output

**Gate:** Reserves increase each turn from controlled factories. Damaged factories produce less.

→ /simplify → commit

### Phase 2: Smuggling Investment Mechanic (~1 session)
**Assigned to:** Gameplay Programmer

Give the RBiH player a strategic lever: invest in smuggling routes to bypass the arms embargo.

- [ ] **3 smuggling routes** defined in `src/sim/economy/smuggling_routes.ts`:
  - **Sarajevo Tunnel** — available from w60+ (historically ~June 1993). Boosts general supply for Sarajevo enclave. Investment accelerates construction.
  - **Adriatic Coast** — available from w0. Croatian port access, needs HRHB alliance > 0. Boosts heavy weapons (small arms, mortars).
  - **Dinaric Spine** — available from w20. Mountain smuggling routes. Unreliable but cheap. Boosts general supply.
- [ ] **Investment mechanic** — player allocates "smuggling points" (derived from negotiation capital or a simple per-turn budget):
  - Each route has capacity (0-100), investment increases capacity by 5-10/turn
  - Capacity × route efficiency → supply bypass per turn
  - Routes can be disrupted (RS interdiction if RS controls adjacent territory)
- [ ] **State field** — `state.military.smuggling_routes: Record<string, { capacity: number, active: boolean, disrupted: boolean }>`
- [ ] **Pipeline step** — `update-smuggling-routes` after supply computation. Adds smuggling income to RBiH reserves.
- [ ] **All factions get equivalent:** RS has "Serbian supply corridor" (Belgrade pipeline, high capacity, degrades with sanctions). HRHB has "Croatian military aid" (HV equipment transfers, degrades during Croat-Bosniak war).
- [ ] **Tests** — smuggling income, route disruption, faction-specific routes

**Gate:** Player can invest in routes. Supply reserves increase accordingly. Route disruption when territory lost.

→ /simplify → commit

## Historical Accuracy Notes

**The economy asymmetry IS the game's strategic texture:**
- **RS** starts with JNA inheritance (500+ tanks) but cannot replace losses. Equipment degrades. Belgrade supply corridor is the lifeline — if sanctions bite (patron pressure), the pipeline shrinks.
- **RBiH** starts with almost nothing (1 tank, 3 artillery per brigade). The entire ARBiH arc is about building from nothing — smuggling, local production, international sympathy. By 1995, they have enough to launch offensives.
- **HRHB** depends on Croatia. When the alliance works, they're well-supplied. During the Croat-Bosniak war (1993-1994), supply routes fragment. Post-Washington, Croatian military aid floods in.

The player should FEEL this asymmetry. RS should feel the slow erosion. RBiH should feel the desperate scrounging. HRHB should feel the dependency.
