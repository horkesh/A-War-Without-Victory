## Context

The v0.3.1 Endgame & Negotiation System implemented the full backend:
- `src/sim/negotiation/` — 10 files: capital computation, peace plans, Dayton negotiation, territorial/institutional packages, bot negotiation, patron events, scoring
- `src/state/` — negotiation_types.ts, negotiation_capital.ts, negotiation_pressure.ts, negotiation_offers.ts, patron_pressure.ts
- War pipeline: `update-patron-ivp`, `update-negotiation-pressure`, `update-negotiation-capital`, `update-negotiation-offers`, patron events, Dayton trigger
- Warroom: DiplomacyModal.ts (443 lines, LIVE), IvpBreakdownModal.ts (132 lines, LIVE)

**What's missing is the player-facing interactive UI** — the player cannot respond to peace plans, negotiate at Dayton, or see capital/patron trends.

## Architectural Decisions (FLAGGED FOR USER REVIEW)

1. **Dayton modal complexity**: The interactive Dayton negotiation could be a simple "accept/reject final terms" or a full multi-round negotiation with counter-proposals. Plan scopes the middle ground: player selects packages → bot responds → final outcome. Full multi-round deferred to v0.6.3 (AI Dynamic Content).

2. **Capital sparklines**: Per-dimension trend history requires storing capital snapshots per turn. If this bloats save files, defer sparklines and show current-only bars. Architect should flag if save size is a concern.

3. **Peace plan timing**: Plans fire at specific weeks (Cutileiro w0, Vance-Owen w40, etc.). If the player already passed the trigger week, the plan is skipped. No retroactive presentation.
