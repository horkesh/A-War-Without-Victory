## Not A Bug

- AWWV is not a conquest sandbox; exhausting all factions is expected.
- The game may report a "bad" outcome even when your faction gained territory.
- The tutorial can be skipped and restarted from settings.
- Sensitive-history events may be represented through consequence, testimony, and reporting rather than player-controlled atrocity actions.
- The same build and same choices should be reproducible; surprising outcomes are not automatically nondeterminism.

## Please Report Immediately

- Crash, freeze, or failure to launch.
- Save that cannot be reloaded.
- Button or modal that blocks all progress.
- Text that appears exploitative, dismissive, or historically wrong.
- Final report/replay mismatch with what happened in the run.
