## One Sentence

**A War Without Victory** is a deterministic historical grand-strategy and operational wargame about the Bosnian War, built around a presidency-level command loop where control gains, exhaustion, displacement, diplomacy, and historical judgment all remain visible.

## Player Fantasy

You are not an omnipotent battlefield god. You are a wartime presidency trying to read an unstable front, manage exhausted institutions, approve or reject dangerous proposals, weigh international pressure, and live with the record the campaign leaves behind.

## Core Loop

1. Read the Warroom, Army HQ briefing, diplomacy packet, and presidential inbox.
2. Inspect the tactical map, command relationships, supply exposure, operation opportunities, and officer/corps state.
3. Decide which proposals, operations, convoys, peace plans, and command warnings deserve presidential attention.
4. Advance the week.
5. Review the aftermath, Chronicle, Cost Ledger, operation records, Codex context, and eventual verdict.

## What Is Playable Now

- Deterministic campaign simulation with reproducible scenario outputs and baseline checks.
- Three-faction Bosnian War campaign structure across RBiH, RS, and HRHB.
- Warroom and Army HQ command surfaces with presidential decision review.
- Tactical map modes for control, contested space, supply reach, authority, and legitimacy.
- Operations, corps/army command constraints, officer identity beats, and post-operation records.
- Turn Aftermath, Chronicle, Dynamic Codex, Cost Ledger, and Pyrrhic verdict framing.
- Read-only Diplomacy panel with active proposals, international pressure, negotiation timeline, needle hints, and actor stance prose.
- Accessibility P0 static closeout, reduced-motion handling, settings surface, and default-off local crash diagnostics with export.
