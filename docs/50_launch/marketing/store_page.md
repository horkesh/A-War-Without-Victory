# Store Page Draft

## Short Description

A deterministic political-military simulation of the Bosnian War where every faction fights through constraint, exhaustion, and consequence.

## Long Description

**A War Without Victory** is a historical grand-strategy and operational wargame about the Bosnian War of 1992-1995. You guide a wartime presidency through military pressure, political legitimacy, humanitarian cost, diplomacy, and command limits while the campaign records what happened and what it cost.

The game is built around a negative-sum thesis: battlefield gains do not erase exhaustion, displacement, political collapse, or historical judgment. The map, Army HQ, Warroom, Chronicle, Codex, Cost Ledger, and verdict systems work together to show not only where control changed, but why it changed and how the war will be remembered.

## Feature Bullets

- Deterministic campaign simulation with reproducible scenario outputs.
- Three-faction Bosnian War command environment with asymmetric historical data and symmetric rule surfaces where possible.
- Presidential decision loop spanning briefings, Army HQ review, operational map inspection, advance-turn aftermath, Chronicle records, and Codex context.
- Tactical map modes for control, contested space, supply reach, authority, and legitimacy.
- Cost Ledger and verdict systems focused on human cost, historical comparison, and Pyrrhic outcomes.
- Dynamic Codex essays that react to already-emitted campaign facts without changing the underlying history text.
- Accessibility-focused UI pass covering semantic controls, form labels, reduced-motion gates, and contrast-token checks.

## Content Warning

This game depicts the Bosnian War, including ethnic cleansing, sieges, displacement, atrocities, political extremism, and civilian suffering. It is a historical simulation, not an endorsement of any faction, ideology, or wartime action.
