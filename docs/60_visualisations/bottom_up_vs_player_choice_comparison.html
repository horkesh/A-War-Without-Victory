11 Analysis

Bottom-Up Mode Is Not Viable for the 40w Calibration Window

The bottom_up recruitment mode produces a fundamentally broken scenario at the 40-week mark. The core problem is systemic:

  • Zero RBiH/HRHB brigades: The militia-to-brigade promotion pipeline never fires. Only 2,205 militia detachments are created alongside 3 army HQs and 9 corps assets, but these militia units cannot issue attack orders or meaningfully defend territory.
  • VRS monopoly: With 77 RS brigades and 0 opposing brigades, the scenario becomes a one-sided walkover. RS barely attacks (9 orders total over 40 weeks) because it faces no organized opposition to trigger offensive behavior.
  • 9.9pp OSID match drop: Overall match falls from 87.6% to 77.7%. The Drina valley is catastrophic (−27.4pp) because VRS never captures the eastern territories it historically seized — the early war Drina Sweep needs brigade-level RS formations that the OOB provides, but without opposing brigades the bot AI doesn’t generate enough attack orders.
  • 96.6% fewer military casualties: 1,138 vs 18,658 — reflecting near-total absence of combat.
  • 96.3% less displacement: 23,897 vs 643,834 — ethnic cleansing mechanics require takeover events that barely occur.

Conclusion: The player_choice mode with bot-driven 3-resource recruitment is the correct configuration for calibration. Bottom-up recruitment needs substantial work on the militia promotion pipeline before it can approach historical fidelity.
