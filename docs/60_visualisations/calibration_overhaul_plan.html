2 — P1: Passive Frontline Attrition
P1 Weekly Attrition for All Front-Line Units Phase A
Problem: Fronts are never asleep. Sniping, shelling, skirmishes, exposure, disease caused steady casualties even without formal attacks. Currently ZERO passive attrition.

Solution: New pipeline step apply-frontline-attrition. Every brigade on a front edge loses personnel per turn.

Formula: attrition = personnel × BASE_ATTRITION_RATE × exposureMod × supplyMod
• BASE_ATTRITION_RATE = 0.005 (0.5%/week)
• Thin front (density <0.5) = 1.5×, dense = 0.7×
• Critical supply = 2.0×, strained = 1.3×

Expected: ~18,750 additional casualties over 40w. Combined with battle KIA (~7k after P9 lethality boost), approaches ~23k target.

Files: New src/sim/combat/frontline_attrition.ts, pipeline in war_phases.ts.

P2 Displacement Kill Rate Calibration Phase A

4 — P3: Population-Driven Recruitment Exhaustion
P3 Tie Recruitment to Remaining Population Phase A Revised
Original: Hard personnel ceilings. Dropped — gamey, scenario-specific.

Revised: Close the population feedback loop. ongoing_mobilization.ts already gates at 20%/35% exhaustion but pool.exhausted is never populated.

Three changes:
1. Recruitment feedback: reinforceBrigadesFromPools() increments pool.exhausted on transfer
2. Casualty feedback: KIA + captured deducted from originating municipality's eligible pop
3. Displacement drain: Fled-abroad permanently lost from recruitment pool

Effect: Each faction hits natural ceiling from demographics. VRS (smaller Serb pop, heavy early recruitment) plateaus ~100k. ARBiH (larger Bosniak pop, slower start) keeps growing past 40w. No caps needed.

7 — P6: ARBiH "Stand and Die"
P6 Faction-Specific Retreat Resistance Phase A
Current: MORALE_RESIST_FLOOR = 70 uniform. ARBiH morale 60 < 70, so never resists.
Fix: ARBiH: 55 (hold ground, die defending homes). VRS: 70 (professional withdrawal). HVO: 65 (middle).
Effect: ARBiH holds territory but takes more casualties. VRS preserves force but cedes ground. Directly produces doctrinal arcs.
8 — P7: War Weariness & Insubordination
P7 Orders Degrade Under Exhaustion Phase B New
Problem: Orders always execute at 100%. Late-war VRS units routinely ignored orders.

Partial execution: Brigade attacks at 70–80% power. Foot-dragging, delayed advance.
Order refusal: (rare) Brigade does not attack. "The men refused to advance."

Triggers:
• exhaustion > 50 + officer_quality < 0.3 → partial execution
• exhaustion > 80 + morale < 40 → refusal

Player notification required. No silent failures. "[301st Bde] executed at reduced effectiveness — war weariness" or "[Bde] refused to advance — troops exhausted."
