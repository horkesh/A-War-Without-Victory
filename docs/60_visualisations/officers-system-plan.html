Two-Tier System Design Summary
Tier 1 (Named Officers, Corps+): Individual officers with 4 ratings (C/A/D/P) on 1-5 scale. Corps commander modifier: 0.90 + competence × 0.04 (range 0.94× to 1.10×). Assignment penalty for incompatible corps.

Tier 2 (Brigade Officer Quality): Abstract officer_quality stat [0.0-1.0] on FormationState. VRS starts 0.65-0.80 (degrades), ARBiH starts 0.10-0.25 (grows ×1.5 rate), HVO stable 0.35-0.50. Brigade modifier: 1.0 + (quality - 0.30) × 0.4.

Combined max: 1.20 (brigade) × 1.10 (corps) = 1.32× (peak VRS brigade under Talić).
Combined min: 0.92 (brigade) × 0.94 (corps) = 0.865× (ARBiH enclave under incompetent cdr).
Key Corrections From Extended Research (7 agents, 500+ sources)
7th Corps (ARBiH): Primary commander was Mehmed Alagić, not Halid Dedić (who was 305th Brigade commander). Alagić founded the 7th Corps (Feb 1994), then returned to the 3rd Corps.

3rd Corps (ARBiH): Missing transitional commander Kadir Jusić (Feb-Sep 1994) between Alagić and Mahmuljin.

4th Corps (ARBiH): Full succession: Pašalić → Budaković "Tetak" → Dreković → Polutak (4 commanders, not 2).

OZ NW Herzegovina (HVO): Full succession: Tole → Šiljeg → Skender → Glasnović (4 commanders). Skender and Glasnović were FFL / international military veterans.

Novica Simić (VRS IBK): Competence upgraded 3→4. Earned command through Op Corridor 92 performance. CoS confirmed: Budimir Gavrić.
