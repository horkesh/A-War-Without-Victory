4 — War-or-Game Realism Audit
P0 — Exhaustion is Dead

The core negative-sum mechanic does not function

All factions show exhaustion 270-400 at w40, but getWarExhaustionTempoMult() only activates at 500-800. No faction reaches the threshold. By January 1993, every real commander felt 9 months of war. Without functioning exhaustion, the sim is a perpetual-motion machine.

Fix direction: Lower thresholds from 500-800 to 200-500, or increase per-turn accumulation so factions hit meaningful range by w25-30.

Blocks negative-sum identity
P1 — HRHB Spectator

HVO fights 1 operation in 40 weeks

HVO fought actively in 1992: Operation Bura around Prozor, Posavina fighting, Mostar battles. 1 order means HRHB holds starting territory and does nothing. A real HVO commander would be relieved of duty.

Fix direction: HRHB patron directive scope fix (already flagged). Investigate aggression floor for HRHB corps.

P1 — 83 Brigades Never Fight

38% of eligible force sits idle in an existential war

In 40 weeks, 83 brigades (38%) see zero combat. Many are front-assigned with sector ownership but never generated or joined an operation. Real ARBiH garrison brigades still had frontline attrition, sniper exchanges, and raids.

Negative-Sum Identity Check

Verdict: NOT YET FELT

The map looks right but the experiential texture is absent. Exhaustion has zero effect. RBiH triples in personnel (mobilization without cost). HRHB's silence is mechanical, not strategic caution. No events force hard choices. The sim is a conventional wargame where the strongest army wins — the opposite of AWWV's design intent.
