Hover Tooltip Demo — visible by default on SECTOR ATTACK above. Full mechanical detail:

Sector Attack

Push on one sector front

Commits 3-8 brigades to attack enemy positions on a single sector. Lowest risk operation type. Objectives are limited to OSIDs within one corps sector.

The corps AI selects the weakest point on the enemy front and concentrates available brigades. Pioneer brigades probe first; reinforcements join when concentration bonus is favorable.

Duration: 4-8 turns typical Brigades: 3-8 committed (MAX_PARTICIPATING = 12) Preparation: 3-7 turns (commander personality) Failure limit: 5 total / 3 consecutive on same target
Best for: Probing weak points, securing specific objectives, testing a sector before committing to a general offensive.

Design Notes

  1. Group-level descriptions tell the player WHAT they are choosing — “Speed vs. casualties tradeoff” is immediately actionable context.
  2. Per-pill subtitles show the TRADEOFF at a glance — the player never has to guess what “Methodical” means relative to “All-Out”.
  3. Hover tooltips provide FULL mechanical explanation — power ratios, durations, casualty modifiers, all visible on demand without leaving the modal.
  4. REGARDLESS gets danger treatment — red background, red pulse animation, warning icon. It is a suicide order and should look like one.
  5. Artillery prep shows COST before enabling — “~200 rounds, +15% attack bonus” lets the player weigh the tradeoff before toggling.
  6. Total added: 15 subtitles + 15 tooltips + 4 group descriptions = 34 pieces of context (was: 0). Zero additional screen space for subtitles; tooltips appear on demand.
