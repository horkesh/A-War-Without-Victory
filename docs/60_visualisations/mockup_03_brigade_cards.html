A: 1 on Card 2 reads as "Attack: 1" — it actually means 1 artillery piece. Completely ambiguous abbreviation.
2
COH 55 FAT 3 is rendered at 7px font, 50% opacity. These are critical combat stats, effectively invisible on screen.
3
~40px wasted space at the bottom of every card. No location, no unit type, no additional context fills this gap.
4
No unit type shown. Is "705th Slavna Mt" mountain infantry? Armor? Motorized? The player cannot tell from the card.
5
No scale on cohesion bar. Is COH 55 good or bad? The bar color (amber) is the only clue. No verbal descriptor, no reference point.
6
Zero hover tooltips. No title attributes on any element. No way to get more detail without opening a separate panel.
