MAX_EVENTS_PER_TURN = 3 Too Restrictive
At w5, 4+ events are eligible (barracks + jna_withdrawal + others). The jna_withdrawal event gets squeezed out, preventing the jna_withdrawn flag from being set. This flag gates drina_cleansing, operation_corridor, and srebrenica_enclave. Fix: increase to 4 or add priority-based selection.
ISSUE-2 · P2
Gorazde Periphery Over-Capture
Offensive paramilitaries take 3 Gorazde municipality OSIDs (glamoc, kamen, sopotnica) that are NOT in the enclave OSID list but should historically remain RBiH. Expand enclave list or remove gorazde from para scope.
ISSUE-3 · P2
corridor_severed Evaluator Data Path
Uses (state as any).derived?.edges which may not exist at runtime. The operational contact graph edges are loaded by the turn pipeline but may not be on the state object. May silently return false (corridor never severed).
