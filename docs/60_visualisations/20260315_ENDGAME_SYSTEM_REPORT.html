§1 System Architecture

The endgame system adds a negotiation layer on top of the existing war simulation. Every turn, the engine tracks what each faction has earned — and what it has cost them.

§2 Implementation Phases
Phase 1 — Capital Tracking
  • negotiation_types.ts — 5 dimensions
  • compute_capital.ts — per-turn engine
  • Territory, humanitarian, credibility, effectiveness, cohesion
  • Faction-specific weighting (ARBiH→humanitarian, RS→territory)
  • 8 tests
Phase 2 — Peace Plans
  • Cutileiro (w0): 44/44/12
  • Vance-Owen (w40): 53/30/17
  • Owen-Stoltenberg (w70): 53/30/17
  • Contact Group (w118): 51/49
  • Bot accept/reject logic + consequences
  • 27 tests
Phase 3 — Patron Pressure
  • Override authority (0-100) per faction
  • Sanctions, territory loss, isolation, defeats
  • Belgrade sanctions RS after Contact Group
  • Srebrenica aftermath + NATO bombing events
  • RS: 10 → 90+ by Dayton (historical arc)
  • 27 tests
Phase 4 — Dayton Negotiation
  • 8 territorial packages (Goražde, Brčko, Posavina...)
  • 6 institutional points (military, presidency...)
  • Bot AI: threshold + patron override scaling
  • Counter-proposals: drop expensive demands first
  • Triggers at w188 or all overrides ≥95
  • 34 tests
Phase 5 — Verdict & Score
  • Pyrrhic Score (0-100, weighted composite)
  • Letter grades A+ through F
  • Historical anchors per faction
  • Full VerdictScreen component
  • Dayton details + statistics
  • 24 tests
Phase 6 — Washington + HV
  • Washington Agreement trigger logic
  • 4 Croatian Army (HV) brigades spawn
  • hv_4th_guards, hv_7th_guards, hv_1st_guards, hv_5th_guards
  • High equipment, western front assignment
  • 6-week preparation delay
  • 14 tests
