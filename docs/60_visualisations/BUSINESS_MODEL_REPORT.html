§6The Honest Version

"I'm building a strategy game about the Bosnian War. It cost us €1,500 in cash and my evenings for 8 weeks. Here's what could happen:

40% chance: It makes less than €4,000. We lose nothing but my time.
20% chance: It makes €4,500-€18,000. Covers costs, validates the idea.
20% chance: It makes €20,000-€50,000. Real supplementary income.
12% chance: It makes €50,000-€150,000. Serious money.
8% chance: It breaks out bigger. But I'm not counting on it.

The break-even is 150 copies. I'll almost certainly clear that. The expected average is ~€50,000, but the most likely single outcome is much less. What I'm NOT doing: quitting my job, taking loans, or investing money we can't afford to lose. The total risk is €1,500 and my time."
