Degradation (Irreversible)

7 War Stories System

At game end, every brigade gets a deterministic narrative summary based on its combat record. Template-driven, no AI/randomness. Six arcs classify the brigade's journey.

Veteran
>65% win rate, >60% personnel retained. "The backbone of the corps."
Bloodied
Heavy combat, heavy losses, still fighting. "The cost of holding the line."
Shattered
<50% peak personnel, >100% cumulative casualties. "A shadow, still in the line."
Risen
>150% cumulative casualties but rebuilt. "Destroyed and reborn." The ARBiH arc.
Destroyed
Formation ceased to exist. "History sealed." Name goes gray in OOB.
Garrison
≤2 battles. Held a quiet sector. "Not every story is written in blood."
Example War Story (Risen arc)

"The 303rd Viteška Mountain was nearly destroyed and rebuilt. Over 14 battles, it suffered 1,847 total casualties — far exceeding its original strength of 780. But it rose each time. By war's end, it stood at 1,640 men, a testament to the will to fight on."

9 Key Design Decisions
History Model
Tallies + Engagement Log
Reconciled Tech Architect (compact tallies only) with Game Designer (full engagement log). Use both: compact tallies for fast queries, FIFO-capped engagement log (200 entries) for war stories.
Decoration Criteria
Criteria-Based, Not Points
Formation Expert proposed point accumulation (Slavna at 40 pts). Game Designer proposed concrete criteria ("5 consecutive wins"). Chose criteria-based — clearer player goals, more narratively satisfying.
Loan Duration
6 Weeks (Not 8)
Formation Expert: 8 weeks. Game Designer: 6 weeks. Chose 6 weeks — shorter deployments create more tension and force more frequent strategic decisions.
Troop Cap Mechanic
Soft + Hard Ceiling
85% soft cap (reinforce ×0.25), 95% hard cap (no reinforce). Formation personnel only — militia pools separate. Solves the ARBiH 177k overproduction.
Decoration Stacking
Highest Tier Only
No stacking across tiers. Tier 3 subsumes tier 1+2 mechanically. Prevents elite brigades from becoming invincible — critical in a negative-sum game.
VRS Decay Method
Equipment Decay, Not Phase Switch
0.5%/week after w26, floor 60%. VRS degradation emerges organically from equipment loss, not from hard-coded phase transitions. Preserves doctrinal arc design rule.
10 Open Questions
  • Q1 Engagement log cap: 200 entries adds ~2.7 MB to saves. Reduce to 50 if save size is a concern? War stories still work with tallies alone.
  • Q2 Tier 3 decorations: Are three tiers too many? Could simplify to two (Slavna/Viteška only + equivalents).
  • Q3 VRS equipment decay floor: 60% — should this be higher (70%) to prevent VRS from becoming unplayable late-game?
  • Q4 Guard brigade timing: HVO guards at weeks 40-48 — too late for a 52-week scenario? Consider 36-44 if year-one only.
  • Q5 Lifecycle events: Should Derventa HVO disband at fixed week 6 (scripted) or when Derventa OSID flips to RS (emergent)? Emergent is more elegant but harder to test.
