11 Consolidated Recommendations

P0 Protect Bihać Pocket

Add defense_terrain_bonus: 0.30 to 5th Corps brigades in OOB data. Alternatively, add Bihać to protected enclave list with hardening mechanic. The 5th Corps pocket held for the entire war — its fall is the single most ahistorical outcome. Consider also adding morale_resist_floor buff for enclave defenders.

P1 Implement Winter Slowdown

Add seasonal modifier reducing attack frequency by 60-80% during weeks 32-39 (November-January). Currently the RS late-war push captures 25 OSIDs when fronts should be frozen. This mechanic is noted as a known gap in calibration — "dead weeks are roughly right but accidental; needs intentional mechanic."

P1 Fix Drina Valley

Višegrad and Zvornik should start RS-controlled or fall in first weeks. Add init_control overrides for Višegrad (7 OSIDs still RBiH). Increase Drina Corps targeting priority for enclaves. Reduce Srebrenica enclave perimeter — 6 excess RBiH OSIDs around the historical enclave.

P2 Review Balanced Doctrine Attack Share

RS attack_share=0.08 in balanced phase gives floor(26*0.08)=2 attack slots per corps — still aggressive. Consider 0.04 (=1 slot) or add a "defensive" phase post-w30 with 0.02. The design intent of "balanced" should mean mostly holding, occasional limited operations.

P2 Fix RBiH Displacement Routing

RBiH civilian fled_abroad=0 is a data gap. Bosniak refugees were the largest displaced group — many fled to Germany, Sweden, Croatia. Review displacement_routing_data.ts for missing Bosniak abroad routes.

P2 Investigate 91% Undefended Attacks

175 of 192 attacks had no defender present. Check local_front_defense.ts militia positioning — are unoccupied OSIDs getting militia defense? If not, RS is essentially capturing empty territory without cost, inflating territorial gains.

P3 Fix Activity Summary Instrumentation

The activity_summary.json reports all-zero metrics despite 40 active war weeks. Weekly report personnel fields also return 0. These are reporting/diagnostic gaps that don't affect simulation but hinder analysis.

P3 Add New Anchor Checks

Consider adding anchors for Višegrad (RS), Lopare (RS), Bijeljina individual OSIDs (RS), and Bihać as a mandatory-PASS anchor. Currently only 14 anchors — expanding to 18-20 would catch more persistent mismatches.
