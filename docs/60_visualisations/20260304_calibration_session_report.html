Key Findings

What Was Discovered — Calibration Lessons

01 / DIRECTION LAW
Override Direction Is Everything
avoided_osids only fix RS over-captures (painted=RBiH/HRHB, sim=RS). RS overrides only fix under-captures (painted=RS, sim=RBiH). Confusing them — as happened in n461 with Donji Vakuf — causes large regressions in the wrong direction. n461 lost −0.7pp area by adding under-captures to avoided_osids.
n461 direction error
02 / CONSOLIDATION WALL
Consolidation Captures Are Unfixable
Cells that flip faction via neighbor consolidation (all neighbors same faction → automatic capture) cannot be fixed by either avoided_osids or HRHB avoided_osids. Confirmed: kakanj:biljesevo, zavidovici:cardak_2, olovo:olovo_2, zvornik:rastosnica_2, and all 4 persistent HRHB over-captures (jablanica:doljani_2, kiseljak:brnjaci_2, etc.) are consolidation-captured.
confirmed n465
03 / CASCADE BARRIER
SE BiH Hard Cascade: Rogatica → Trnovo → Sarajevo
Any RS gain in southeastern BiH (Rogatica 5 cells, Cajniče 2 cells, Foča perimeter) causes trnovo:delijas to flip RS via consolidation cascade, collapsing SARAJEVO match by 8+ percentage points. Confirmed 3 times: n438, n441, n455. Also confirmed: Cajniče → POSAVINA_NE cascade (−1.5pp Zvornik area). Structural barrier — no override-based fix possible.
structural — no fix
04 / LOAD-BEARING WRONG CAPTURE
turbe_2 RS Over-Capture Enables Donji Vakuf Cascade
In n462, VRS capturing travnik:turbe_2 (a wrong RS over-capture) was the stepping stone enabling consolidation to cascade and fix 3 Donji Vakuf cells (jemanlici, korenici, prusac_2). Fixing turbe_2 with avoided_osids (n463) broke the entire Donji Vakuf chain: 3 correct cells reverted, new Travnik mis-captures appeared. Net −0.7pp. "Wrong captures" can be load-bearing.
do not fix turbe_2
05 / FRAGILE DEPENDENCY
Kalesija → Kupres Bonus: VRS Force Dynamics
Adding kalesija:seher_2 + gojcin_2 RS overrides (n466) produced a bonus fix of kupres:kupres_2 — HERZEGOVINA jumped from 94.5% to 96.8%. The mechanism: VRS corps with Kalesija secured redirected forces toward Kupres, enabling them to hold kupres_2 against HRHB pressure. Adding Kladanj overrides on top (n467) disrupted this VRS allocation and kupres_2 reverted. The dependency is real but fragile.
n466 ATH
06 / EBK REDIRECT
Doboj Avoided → EBK Redirects to Zvornik
When RS avoided_osids prevent the East Bosnia Corps (EBK) from targeting Doboj cells, it redirects to Zvornik area, causing disorganization: cells VRS should hold (donja_kamenica, krizevici) are lost, while wrong cells (rastosnica_2) are over-captured. n457 fixed the Doboj side (+3.9pp CENTRAL_BOSNIA) but POSAVINA_NE dropped −2.4pp. n459 partially fixed POSAVINA_NE with explicit Zvornik overrides.
mitigated in n459
07 / RS-HRHB PENALTY
RS Attack Penalty Hurts Large Regions
Testing RS-HRHB attack penalties (−80 in n453, −200 in n450, −400 in n449) consistently caused KRAJINA −1.5–3.8pp and HERZEGOVINA −1.1–1.9pp regressions. The mechanism: HVO brigades face reduced VRS pressure → run unchallenged → expand into RS territory. Even small penalties hurt. Central Bosnia gains (~+2pp) were never enough to offset large-region losses. Penalty disabled at 0.
disabled — do not re-enable
Mechanic Changes

Engine Changes Made This Session

New Mechanic

ARBiH Homeland Last Stand

Bosniak defenders in co-ethnic majority OSIDs (≥50%) absorb both 'victory' and 'costly_victory' attack outcomes — no retreat. Implemented in attack_resolution_osid.ts. Reflects historically documented ARBiH fighting spirit: defenders fighting for their homes accepted heavy casualties rather than giving ground. Lowering the threshold to 0.60 (from 0.50) was tested in n443 — zero effect, as no contested cells had 0.50–0.60 Bosniak share.

Before
Costly victory → retreat
After
≥50% Bosniak → absorb
Tuned

Morale Resist Floors

Morale ≥ floor + costly_victory outcome → defender absorbs (no retreat). Lowering RBiH floor increases resistance — ARBiH units hold longer even at moderate morale. HRHB floor also reduced for similar effect. Implemented in getMoraleResistFloor() in combat_math.ts.

Before
RBiH=62, HRHB=65
After
RBiH=50, HRHB=60
